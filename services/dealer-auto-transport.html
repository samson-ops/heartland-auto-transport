Heartland Auto Transport partners with new car dealerships, used car lots, and franchise dealers across Alabama and nationwide to handle the constant flow of vehicles between dealer locations, auction yards, and inventory sources. We understand dealer economics: every day a vehicle isn't on your lot is a day you can't sell it. What Heartland's Dealer Auto Transport for Dealerships Includes

Dealer Trade Coordination

Dealer-to-dealer trades happen constantly in the franchise world — a customer wants a specific color, trim, or option package, and the dealer locates one at a sister store hundreds of miles away. We coordinate these trades with both sides of the transaction, handle pickup and delivery on the dealer's schedule, and provide tracking visibility for both buying and selling dealers throughout transit.

Auction Pickup Service

Manheim, Adesa, ACV, and independent auction yards have strict pickup deadlines — typically 5-7 days after sale, with daily storage fees after. We dispatch carriers to auction yards quickly, handle the gate paperwork and release procedures, and deliver vehicles directly to your dealership or detail facility.

Wholesale Dealer Pricing

Dealer rates differ from retail rates. Dealers shipping multiple vehicles per month qualify for volume pricing, dedicated dispatcher relationships, and streamlined billing. Whether you ship 5 vehicles a month or 50, we'll work out pricing that reflects your actual volume.

Lot Replenishment

New car dealers receive factory deliveries on transporters, but used car lots often need ongoing replenishment from auctions, trade-ins from sister stores, and inventory acquired across state lines. We handle these regular shipments with predictable scheduling so your inventory pipeline stays full.

Specific Services Offered

  • Open transport for standard inventory — the most cost-effective option for the vast majority of dealer trades and auction pickups
  • Enclosed transport for high-value units — luxury, exotic, classic, and any vehicle where lot-arrival appearance matters more than the cost difference
  • Multi-vehicle moves — shipping 2-10 vehicles simultaneously, with combined pricing that beats individual shipments
  • Auction-specific dispatch — drivers familiar with Manheim, Adesa, Copart, IAA, and ACV procedures and gate processes
  • Door-to-door dealer service — we deliver directly to your dealership, detail facility, or front line

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you handle dealer trades between stores?

Constantly — same-brand swaps and wholesale moves alike. Standard trades dispatch in 1–3 days; if a sold customer is waiting, tell us and we'll expedite the lane.

Can you move multiple units from an auction to our lot?

Yes, and consolidating saves real money — a 3-car pull from the same auction on one carrier prices far better than three singles. Give us the run list and we'll build the load.

How do you handle plates, keys, and paperwork?

Keys travel with the vehicle; paperwork shouldn't. The driver documents condition at pickup, your receiving store inspects and signs at delivery, and titles move separately through your normal channels.

Do you offer volume pricing for dealerships?

Yes — recurring dealer accounts get lane-consistent pricing, priority dispatch, and one point of contact who knows your stores. Most of our dealer relationships started with a single trade.
