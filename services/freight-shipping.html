What Heartland's Freight Shipping Includes

Full Truckload (FTL)

Full truckload service dedicates an entire trailer to your shipment. The truck goes from origin to destination without consolidation, making FTL faster than LTL for large shipments and ideal when freight needs to stay together or arrive on tight timelines.

Less-Than-Truckload (LTL)

LTL shipping moves smaller freight (typically 150-15,000 pounds) by consolidating multiple customers' freight on a single carrier. LTL pricing is per-pound or per-pallet, making it cost-effective for shipments too small to warrant a dedicated truck.

Expedited Freight

When standard transit times aren't fast enough, expedited freight prioritizes your shipment with dedicated routing and faster transit. Premium pricing applies, but the time savings often justify it for urgent freight.

Specialty Freight

Beyond standard freight, specialty categories include refrigerated (reefer), temperature-controlled, hazardous materials (hazmat), high-value goods, and oversize cargo. Each requires specialized carriers and handling. We coordinate the right specialty service for each load.

Frequently Asked Questions

What counts as vehicle freight versus standard car shipping?

Anything beyond a single driveable car: crated motorcycles, palletized ATVs, parts loads, mixed shipments of vehicles and equipment, or dedicated-trailer moves. The equipment and pricing differ from slot-on-a-9-car shipping.

Can you combine vehicles and parts in one shipment?

Often yes — a car plus a crated engine, or a truck with a palletized attachment. Contents must be properly crated or palletized and declared upfront so the carrier's insurance and securement plan cover the whole load.

Do you handle LTL-style shared loads for vehicles?

That's what multi-car open transport is — shared trailer space priced per unit. For freight that can't share (oversize, crated, sensitive), we quote dedicated or partial-dedicated trailers instead.

How is vehicle freight priced?

By space, weight, lane, and handling requirements. Shared-space loads price per unit; dedicated trailers price per mile. Crating, liftgate, and dock requirements are itemized in the quote, never discovered at pickup.
