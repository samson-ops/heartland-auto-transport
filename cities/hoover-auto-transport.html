Local Questions

Hoover Auto Transport FAQs

Will my company's relocation program work with you?
Almost certainly — we've processed moves through most major relocation administrators and lump-sum programs. Send the program details with your quote request; we format the invoice to fit up front, with direct billing where allowed.
Can one household ship enclosed and open vehicles together?
It's the Hoover standard. The garage piece rides covered while the daily fleet ships open, on coordinated schedules landing the same week, with one dispatcher owning the whole set.
How does Bluff Park pickup work?
Below the mountain — the crest streets exceed a loaded trailer's safe limits, so Bluff Park meets at the Patton Chapel or Highway 31 lots five minutes down. Drivers plan it automatically.
What does Hoover to Denver cost for a corporate transfer?
About $950 to $1,300 with 5 to 6 day transit. Employer-reimbursed moves get administrator-formatted documentation as standard, and delivery times to your start date.
Do multi-car families really save by shipping together?
Yes — 10 to 20 percent per vehicle when the fleet shares one carrier, which Hoover's summer moves almost always can. One pickup, one delivery, one smaller bill.
What happens during SEC tournament week at the Met?
Galleria-area staging slows, so that week's pickups shift to the Highway 150 corridors automatically. If your dates brush the tournament, we route around it at booking for free.
Can you receive an auction car to my Greystone garage?
Yes — enclosed and documented, dispatched inside the sale's free-storage window with release paperwork handled, and delivery coordinated to your gate and schedule.
How do August college shipments from Hoover work?
In cohorts. Graduates head to the same campuses in groups, families bound for the same schools share carriers at a discount, and we text the students directly for the handoffs. Book three weeks ahead of move-in.
Is Hoover priced the same as Birmingham?
Yes — one metro market, one rate table. Riverchase to Trussville, the route sets the price, not the suburb.
Is my car insured during shipping?
Yes. Every carrier holds FMCSA licensing and cargo insurance, and the signed pickup record is your evidence. Compare the car against it at delivery before signing off.
What if a car in our fleet doesn't run?
Winch loading adds $150 to $250 for that vehicle, and it can still share the carrier with the runners. Tell us at booking what it can do and the right equipment comes.
When is the cheapest time to ship from Hoover?
Early spring and fall, between the corporate quarters and the school calendar. January and February hit the absolute floor, with next-day dispatch the norm.
Can you ship my student's car and my transfer vehicles in one plan?
Yes — August families do it every year. The college car heads to campus while the household fleet moves to the new city, sequenced by one dispatcher so nothing collides and every shared-carrier discount gets caught.
Do you handle estate vehicles from Hoover?
With care and documentation — full photos at pickup, executor-paced scheduling, and delivery to heirs anywhere in the country. Enclosed transport is available for anything of collector or sentimental value.
How fast are Hoover pickups?
Usually 1 to 2 business days. I-65 and I-459 bracket the city, so carriers reach the staging corners without crossing the metro — Hoover often dispatches as fast as Birmingham proper.
Do you ship motorcycles from Hoover?
Yes — wheel-chocked on open carriers or crated enclosed for the special ones, priced well under car rates. Barber-event traffic keeps the route experienced.
Where do Trace Crossings and Green Valley pickups happen?
Mostly at the door — both neighborhoods' streets handle the trailer in the flatter sections. Where a cul-de-sac or grade gets tight, the driver names the nearest retail corner at booking, usually along Highway 150 or Patton Chapel, and the plan is locked before pickup day.
