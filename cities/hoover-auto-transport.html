Local Questions

Hoover Auto Transport FAQs

Where do carriers meet in Hoover?
Most loads stage at the Riverchase Galleria perimeter lots — drivers like the straight shot from I-459 and the space to work. For Ross Bridge and Lake Cyrus, the Highway 150 retail centers are closer; Greystone and Inverness meet along US-280.
Can a carrier get into Bluff Park?
The mountain streets are steep and winding, so full-size rigs don't climb them. Bluff Park pickups meet at the bottom of the hill — usually the Patton Chapel or Highway 31 lots — a five-minute drive from most homes.
Is Hoover pricing the same as Birmingham?
Effectively yes. Carriers treat the metro as one market, so Hoover shipments price off the same lane rates. Occasionally a driver already staged in Hoover will bid a touch lower to fill a spot without crossing town.
Does my company's relocation program work with you?
Almost certainly — we've processed moves through most major relocation administrators and lump-sum programs. Send the program details with your quote request and we'll match the invoice format upfront, with direct billing where the program allows. Reimbursement becomes a formality instead of a project.
Can you ship one car enclosed and two open for the same move?
Routinely — the executive-family pattern. The daily drivers ride open at metro rates while the garage car travels enclosed, on coordinated schedules so the whole fleet lands the same week. One dispatcher owns the set.
How does Bluff Park pickup work?
Below the mountain — the crest streets exceed what a loaded rig can safely climb, so Bluff Park loads meet at the Patton Chapel or Highway 31 lots five minutes down. The staging is established enough that drivers plan it automatically.
What happens during SEC tournament week at the Met?
Galleria-area staging slows, so we shift that week's pickups to the Highway 150 corridors. If your dates brush the tournament, we'll route around it at booking — no premium, just planning.
What does Hoover to Charlotte cost for a corporate transfer?
Typically $550–$750 for standard vehicles with 2–3 day transit — a well-traveled Southeast headquarters lane. Employer-reimbursed moves get program-formatted documentation as standard.
Do you handle auction purchases arriving to Greystone?
Regularly — national auction buys ship into the 280 corridor's garages year-round, enclosed for the collector grade. Give us the sale's release requirements and we'll dispatch inside the storage window with delivery coordinated to your gate.
How do college shipments from Hoover's school zones work?
Each August a wave of well-equipped student vehicles leaves Hoover for campuses nationwide — we coordinate delivery with move-in weekends, communicate directly with the student for the handoff, and share-carrier match Hoover families heading to the same universities. Book three weeks ahead of August; the school zones ship in cohorts.
