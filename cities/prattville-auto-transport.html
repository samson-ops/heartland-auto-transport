Local Questions

Prattville Auto Transport FAQs

How do Maxwell military moves work from Prattville?
River-north, start to finish. We stage at the Cobbs Ford corridor so you skip the cross-city drive, time pickup to your orders, and move your dates free when the Air Force amends them. Paperwork comes formatted for the reimbursement system.
How fast can you pick up a car in Prattville?
Usually 1 to 3 business days. Exit 179 makes Prattville one of the easiest loads on I-65 — carriers grab it without touching Montgomery traffic, which sometimes beats the big city's own dispatch times.
What does Prattville to Birmingham cost?
About $250 to $375 for a standard car — 85 miles straight up I-65 with next-day delivery routine. It's one of the most liquid short routes in the state.
Where does the truck meet me if I live in Old Prattville?
On the Main Street commercial stretch, about three minutes from the creekside blocks. The historic downtown streets are too narrow for the truck, so that short hop keeps loading quick and the district untouched.
Is Millbrook the same price as Prattville?
Yes — the river region north of Montgomery ships as one market. Millbrook and Marbury quote identically, and the driver picks whichever staging corner is closest to you.
Can we ship both family cars together?
Yes, and you should — two cars on one truck save 10 to 20 percent each. June movers especially: one pickup, one delivery, one smaller bill during the busiest season.
Do state government relocations get special paperwork?
They get the right paperwork — an itemized invoice that fits agency reimbursement rules, plus delivery timed to your appointment or session start date. Tell us the date as soon as you know it.
Can you ship our golf cart to the beach for the season?
Yes, it's a Prattville regular. Panhandle runs cost $350 to $525 for cars and much less for carts. Book the trip down and the trip back together and save over two separate orders.
Is my car insured while it ships?
Yes. Every carrier is FMCSA-licensed with cargo insurance. The driver documents condition at pickup, you verify at delivery, and the time in between is covered.
What if my car doesn't start?
We ship non-runners with winch loading for about $150 to $250 extra. Just tell us at booking whether it rolls, steers, and brakes so the right truck shows up.
When is the cheapest time to ship from Prattville?
Late fall through February, when the government and school calendars both go quiet. Flexible dates in those months catch the year's lowest prices on every route.
How should I prepare the car?
Remove personal items, leave about a quarter tank of gas, turn off alarms and toll tags, and give it a wash so the condition photos are honest and clear. Keys and ID at the meetup — that's everything.
Can you hold my car until our closing goes through?
Yes. We stage at Cobbs Ford or Highpoint and hand off when your keys are ready. A day or two of flex is normal in this market and usually costs nothing.
Can a Maxwell household and a Capitol job household coordinate one move?
Yes — two-calendar families are a Prattville specialty. We sequence both departures as one project, stage everything river-north at Cobbs Ford, and thread the timing between the Air Force's dates and the state's, so neither employer's schedule wrecks the other's.
Do you ship motorcycles from Prattville?
Yes. Bikes ride wheel-chocked and strapped on open carriers, or crated in enclosed trailers for the special ones, priced well under car rates because they use less deck space.
