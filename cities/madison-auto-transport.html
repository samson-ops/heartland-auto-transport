Tips for Shipping a Car in Madison

Relocating for Mazda Toyota? Book when you accept the offer, not when you start packing. Onboarding classes bring in dozens of families at once, and the Michigan and Ohio routes fill around them. We deliver during your arrival week and can hold the car at Town Madison until your closing goes through.

The Detroit pipeline runs both ways. Plenty of Madison families ship a car north for family visits, transfers back, or a student headed to a Michigan school. Standard cars on the Detroit route run about $700 to $950.

Defense and contractor moves are our other Madison staple. We work around clearance timelines and report dates, and if your agency or company shifts your start date — they do — your pickup shifts free.

Shipping an EV? Madison has one of the highest electric-car rates in Alabama, and we ship them constantly. Charge the battery to between 30 and 80 percent, tell us the model so the driver knows the transport-mode steps, and it rides like any other car.

Multi-car household? That's the Madison standard. Two or three family vehicles on the same truck save 10 to 20 percent per car — one pickup, one delivery, one bill.

Brand-new subdivision? Not a problem, just a conversation. Tell us the neighborhood name and we'll confirm which entrance works for a 75-foot truck. If the streets are still under construction, the Town Madison lots are ten minutes from everywhere.

Nashville is the bargain route — 105 miles up I-65, usually $300 to $450, often next-day. If you're comparing shipping to driving, this route makes shipping the easy winner once you count your time.

The quiet months are late winter and early fall. Between hiring waves and school-year moves, Madison routes dispatch next-day at the year's lowest prices.

Local Questions

Madison Auto Transport FAQs

How fast can you pick up a car in Madison?
Usually 1 to 2 business days — among the fastest in the state. I-565's exits let carriers grab Madison loads without leaving their route, and the Huntsville metro keeps trucks nearby.
I'm moving from Michigan for Mazda Toyota. How should I plan?
Book the day you accept the offer. The Detroit-area route runs weekly at $700 to $950, we deliver during your arrival week, and plant relocation programs reimburse against our itemized invoice.
My subdivision is so new it's not on GPS. Will the driver find it?
Yes, because we confirm the details with you at booking — the neighborhood name, which entrance is paved, where construction gates sit. If the streets aren't ready, Town Madison is the ten-minute backup.
Do you ship Teslas and other EVs?
All the time — Madison ships more EVs than almost anywhere in Alabama. Charge to 30–80 percent, tell us the model, and the driver handles transport mode. Everything else works exactly like a gas car.
Can all our family cars go on one truck?
Usually yes, and it saves 10 to 20 percent per car. Multi-car households are Madison's normal move. One dispatcher handles the whole set with one pickup and one delivery.
What does Madison to Nashville cost?
About $300 to $450 — 105 miles on a route carriers run daily. Next-day delivery is common, and flexible dates catch the low end of the range.
My contractor start date moved. Does that cost me?
No. Defense and contractor schedules shift all the time, and our dispatchers reschedule Madison moves free. Tell us the new date and the plan updates the same day.
Is Madison priced the same as Huntsville?
Yes — one metro, one price list. Madison sometimes dispatches a bit faster because carriers can load right off I-565 without crossing the metro.
Is my car insured on the trip?
Yes. Every carrier is FMCSA-licensed and carries cargo insurance. Condition is documented at pickup, verified at delivery, and covered in between.
How do I prepare my car?
Remove personal items, leave about a quarter tank of gas, turn off alarms and toll tags, and wash it so the inspection photos are clear. Have your keys at the meetup.
Can you hold my car if our closing gets delayed?
Yes. Closings slip in a market this fast, so we stage cars at the Town Madison lots and hand off when your keys are ready — usually at no extra cost for a day or two.
Do you serve Triana and the south side of the interstate?
Yes, at the same Madison rates. South-side pickups meet along the Zierdt Road corridor or at Town Madison, minutes north.
Do you ship cars for Redstone and Research Park workers who live in Madison?
Every week. Carriers can't enter the Arsenal, so on-post pickups meet outside Gate 9 off I-565 — ten minutes from most of Madison. Research Park workers usually meet at Town Madison or the Bridge Street area, whichever is closer to home.
What's the cheapest time to ship from Madison?
Late winter, especially late January through February. Between hiring waves, carriers have open space and drop prices to fill it. Flexible dates in those weeks catch the year's best rates.
