Daphne anchors the Eastern Shore at the exact point where I-10 meets US-98, which makes it the most carrier-accessible city in booming Baldwin County. Retirees arriving from the Midwest, professionals commuting across the bay to Mobile, and snowbirds cycling seasonally all ship through Daphne, and the interstate junction means drivers rarely deadhead more than a mile to load.

Local Questions

Daphne Auto Transport FAQs

Why is Daphne such an easy pickup for carriers?
Geography. I-10 and US-98 intersect right at the city, so a driver running the Gulf corridor can exit, load at Jubilee Square, and be back on the interstate in ten minutes. That convenience shows up in competitive bids.
Do you run snowbird routes from Daphne?
All season long — October through April, the Eastern Shore-to-Midwest lanes (Ohio, Michigan, Indiana, Illinois) are among our busiest. Book two weeks ahead in peak months; prices rise as the season deepens.
Can carriers get into Lake Forest?
The interior streets are too winding for a loaded trailer, so Lake Forest pickups meet at the US-98 entrance. It's a two-minute drive from most homes in the neighborhood.
