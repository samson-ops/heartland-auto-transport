Local Questions

Daphne Auto Transport FAQs

When should snowbirds book Eastern Shore shipments?
Two weeks ahead in season — October through December southbound and mid-March through April northbound. The Midwest routes genuinely fill, and early booking gets carrier choice at pre-peak prices. Book the round trip as a pair and save more.
Why can't the truck come down my oak-canopy street?
The live oaks meet overhead at heights a loaded carrier can't clear, and nobody's trimming those trees. Canopy addresses stage at Jubilee Square or the 98 corridor, five minutes away, and the oaks stay perfect.
Can you gather cars from multiple states to my new Daphne home?
Yes — new-arrival consolidation is a regular request here. We sequence pickups from each origin and land the deliveries in the same week, so your whole fleet reunites at once.
Do you ship golf carts to the cart-friendly neighborhoods?
Constantly. Carts ride strapped on open decks or enclosed for the pristine ones, priced well under car rates, with gatehouse access arranged in advance where needed.
Should my garage-kept car ship enclosed to the coast?
If the finish matters, yes. Open decks collect a salt film on the bay approaches, and enclosed delivery lands a pristine car exactly as it left. Daily drivers ride open happily; showpieces get the trailer with walls.
Is Spanish Fort the same price as Daphne?
Yes — the Eastern Shore quotes as one market. Spanish Fort stages at the Causeway-side exits and Daphne at Jubilee Square, but the route sets the price either way.
What happens if a hurricane threatens during my shipment?
We watch the Gulf all season and may move your pickup a day to stay ahead of weather — with a call to you first, always, and never an extra charge for it.
Can you time delivery to my closing date?
Yes, and we flex when closings slip — a day or two is free and normal here. For longer gaps, the Jubilee Square meetup hands you the car wherever you're staying meanwhile.
What does Daphne to New Orleans cost?
About $350 to $500 with next-day delivery — 160 miles on I-10's constant traffic keeps it one of the most liquid routes on the coast.
Is my car insured across the trip?
Yes. Carriers hold FMCSA licenses and cargo insurance, and your signed pickup condition form is the record. Match the car against it at delivery before signing.
What if the car won't start?
Winch loading covers it for about $150 to $250 more. Tell us at booking whether it rolls, steers, and brakes, and the correct equipment arrives with the truck.
How do I get the car ready to ship?
Take out personal items, keep about a quarter tank of fuel, turn off the alarm and toll tag, and wash it so its condition photographs honestly. Keys and ID at the handoff finish the list.
How fast are Eastern Shore pickups outside snowbird season?
Usually 1 to 3 business days. The I-10/98 junction keeps carriers minutes away, and off-season Daphne loads often dispatch same-week without any planning at all.
Can you ship an estate vehicle to an heir out of state?
Yes, with the patience these moves deserve. We coordinate with the executor on timing, photograph the car thoroughly at pickup, and walk the receiving family member through their first delivery. Enclosed transport is available for anything irreplaceable.
Do you move boat-tow trucks and SUVs ahead of the season?
Yes — the tow vehicle ships like any car, timed ahead of your boat's season. The boat itself rides your hitch; we position what pulls it.
