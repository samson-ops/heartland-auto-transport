Tips for Shipping a Car in Fairhope

Decide open or enclosed by the garage, not the price sheet. The daily SUV rides open without a second thought. The garage-kept convertible, the inherited classic, the concours candidate — those ride enclosed with soft straps and a photo record. Their owners never regret it.

Spring is closing season on the bluff. Inbound moves peak April through June as northern sellers become southern buyers. The snowbird overlay adds October-through-December and March-through-April waves on the same Midwest routes. Two weeks' notice in those windows keeps your pick of carriers.

Renovating before move-in? Common here. We deliver the practical car at closing. The enclosed delivery holds until your contractor hands over the garage. The showpiece shouldn't arrive before its room does.

Local Questions

Fairhope Auto Transport FAQs

Should my garage-kept car ship enclosed to Fairhope?
If the finish matters to you, yes. Between coastal salt film and the caliber of car that retires here, enclosed is the Fairhope default for showpieces — soft straps, full photos, 60 to 80 percent over open rates, and usually worth every dollar.
How do estate vehicle shipments work?
On the executor's timeline, with care. We photograph the car completely at pickup, deliver to heirs anywhere in the country, and walk first-time recipients through the delivery. Enclosed transport and auction-house delivery are both available.
Why does my street need a meetup instead of door-to-door?
The oaks. The canopy over the bluff lanes and older blocks meets below trailer height, and nobody's touching those trees. The Greeno Road meetup is five minutes out and confirmed the day before.
How does Point Clear pickup work?
At the Highway 98 junctions, minutes from the Scenic 98 addresses — the canopy and resort traffic rule out carrier access on the scenic road itself. The driver confirms the exact corner when scheduling.
What does the Chicago route cost to Fairhope?
About $750 to $1,000 for standard vehicles, firming in snowbird season and softening to the floor in late winter. Enclosed adds its usual premium — a common choice given what makes this trip.
Can you hold the showpiece until my garage renovation finishes?
Yes — that's a standard Fairhope sequence. Practical car at closing, enclosed delivery when the contractor hands over the space. We time it to the garage, not the calendar.
Do you ship golf carts to the bluff neighborhoods?
Regularly. Carts ride strapped on open decks or enclosed for the showpieces, priced well under car rates, delivered to Fairhope's cart-friendly blocks every season.
Can you ship a collector car I sold to an out-of-state buyer?
Yes, and enclosed delivery with a photo record protects the sale for both sides. Many sellers here quote delivered pricing to reach buyers nationwide — we make that painless.
Is my car insured during transport?
Yes. Every carrier holds FMCSA licensing and cargo insurance, and the signed photo condition report from pickup is your record. Verify against it at delivery before signing.
How fast are Fairhope pickups?
Usually 1 to 3 business days off-season, and two weeks' notice keeps that pace during the spring closing rush and snowbird windows. Enclosed carriers book a few days further out than open ones.
What if the car doesn't run?
Winch loading adds about $150 to $250, and for low or delicate vehicles we send a liftgate-equipped enclosed rig. Describe the car honestly at booking and the right equipment shows up.
When is the cheapest time to ship here?
Late January, February, and early September — the same Midwest route that firmed up for the snowbirds prices at its floor then, and carriers have room on short notice.
Can you consolidate cars from several states to my new Fairhope home?
Yes — new arrivals often have a car in storage somewhere and another at a family place. We sequence pickups from each origin so everything lands the same week, open and enclosed loads coordinated by one dispatcher.
What's the Arts Festival week effect on shipping?
Downtown fills for the March weekend, so that week's pickups shift up Greeno automatically at no charge. Festival-bound artwork vehicles and vendor rigs book a couple weeks ahead — the arts calendar concentrates them.
