Local Know-How

Staging Around Enterprise and the Fort

Carriers can't enter Fort Novosel, so gate-adjacent staging is standard: the Boll Weevil Circle retail corridor serves most of the city, and the Rucker Boulevard centers work for families closest to the Daleville gate. Downtown pickups near the Circle load easily; newer subdivisions off Highway 27 meet at the corridor lots.

Local Questions

Enterprise Auto Transport FAQs

How do Fort Novosel vehicle pickups work?
Carriers stage outside the installation — usually the Boll Weevil Circle corridor or Rucker Boulevard centers near the Daleville gate. We time pickup to your orders, and if the Army shifts your date, we shift with it at no charge.
Can you deliver to my next duty station?
Yes — duty-station-to-duty-station is our core Enterprise service. Lanes to Fort Cavazos, Fort Liberty, Fort Campbell, and the West Coast bases run regularly, and we can hold delivery until you've signed for housing.
When do Enterprise lanes get busiest?
Flight-school graduation weeks and summer PCS season. If you know your orders window, book as soon as dates firm up — graduation-week slots fill fast.
