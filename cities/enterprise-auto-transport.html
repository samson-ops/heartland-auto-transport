Auto Transport in Enterprise, Alabama

Ship your car to or from Enterprise, home of Army Aviation — PCS moves timed to your orders, with paperwork built for reimbursement.

Fully Insured (334) 423-3997

Every Army helicopter pilot trains at Fort Novosel, and that makes little Enterprise a national shipping crossroads. Families arrive from every state for flight school. They live here about eighteen months, then PCS out to bases across the country. Heartland Auto Transport runs those moves week in and week out. We also ship for instructor pilots, contractors, and the civilian families who call Coffee County home for good.

If this is your first PCS with a shipped vehicle, here's the shape of it. You book with your orders in hand. Heartland Auto Transport assigns a licensed, insured carrier. Pickup gets staged around your final-out date. The driver records the car's condition on a signed form. The car rides to your gaining installation. Delivery waits until you've signed for housing if it needs to. Your invoice and that condition form are the two papers the military reimbursement system wants.

Local Know-How

How Car Pickup Works in Enterprise

Carriers can't enter Fort Novosel, so every pickup happens off post. The standard meeting spots are the Boll Weevil Circle shopping corridor and the Rucker Boulevard centers. One piece of local timing: the gate roads surge at 6:30 a.m. and again around 4:30 p.m. on training days, so a mid-morning meetup is the smooth play. Families living on post just build an extra fifteen minutes into the drive out — the plan is set before the truck ever rolls.

Local Insight

Tips for Shipping a Car in Enterprise

Book the day orders drop. That's the single best money move at Novosel. Graduation weeks pack dozens of PCS moves into a town with a limited number of trucks. Early bookings choose carriers at normal prices. Late ones scramble.

Orders amended? So is your schedule, free of charge. Report dates move constantly here, and our dispatchers treat a date change as Tuesday, not an emergency. Call or text the new dates and the plan updates the same day.

Dual-military couple with two cars? Tell us both sets of orders. When the routes line up, both vehicles ride one carrier — one meetup on each end, and per-vehicle pricing that beats two separate bookings.

Going overseas? For OCONUS orders we deliver to the vehicle processing center with the car prepped to their standards: about a quarter tank, cleaned, personal items out, alarm off. The condition report travels with it for your records.

Motorcycles ship constantly from here — aviation attracts riders. Bikes ride wheel-chocked on open carriers or crated in enclosed trailers for the prized machines, priced well under car rates, to any installation in the country.

Keeping one car for the drive? That's the standard family play: ship the second vehicle, drive the first, and arrive with both inside the same week. Delivery can hold at the gaining base until housing assigns.

Incoming to flight school? The pipeline runs both ways. We deliver from your last posting during your arrival week and stage at the Circle until you have keys — the car needs you, not a finished address.

Daleville, Level Plains, and New Brockton all ship at Enterprise rates. The whole Novosel orbit quotes as one market, and gate-adjacent staging serves on-post households without the traffic gauntlet.

Local Questions

Enterprise Auto Transport FAQs

How does a PCS car shipment from Fort Novosel work?
Book with orders in hand. We stage pickup at the Boll Weevil Circle corridor before your final-out, hold delivery at the gaining base until housing assigns, and the paperwork comes formatted for the military system. Amended orders move your dates free.
What does shipping to Joint Base Lewis-McChord cost after graduation?
About $1,250 to $1,600 for a standard vehicle, 7 to 10 days coast to coast. It's one of our signature Enterprise routes, and early booking around graduation weeks makes a real price difference.
How does PPM reimbursement paperwork work with you?
You get an itemized invoice and the signed condition form — the two documents the military moving system asks for. Thousands of Novosel families have filed ours successfully. Keep both with your orders packet.
My orders changed a week before pickup. Now what?
The pickup moves with them at no charge. Date changes are the weather at Novosel, and our dispatchers reschedule without penalty. Send the new dates and the plan updates the same day.
Can dual-military couples ship both cars together?
When the route allows, yes — one carrier, one meetup at each end, and per-vehicle pricing below two separate bookings. Send us both sets of orders and we'll sequence them.
Can you ship my motorcycle to my next duty station?
Constantly. Bikes ride wheel-chocked on open carriers or crated in enclosed trailers for high-value machines, priced well under car rates, to any installation in the country.
Do you deliver to the vehicle processing center for overseas orders?
Yes. For OCONUS moves we deliver to the designated VPC with the car prepped to their documented standards — quarter tank, cleaned, personal items out, alarm disabled — and you keep the condition report.
Where exactly do on-post families meet the carrier?
Just outside the installation at the Boll Weevil Circle or Rucker Boulevard corners — carriers can't come through the gate. Mid-morning meetups dodge the training-day gate surges.
How fast can you pick up in Enterprise?
Usually 2 to 4 business days. Carriers reach the Wiregrass on US-84 and Highway 27 daily, but graduation weeks concentrate demand — which is why booking at orders beats booking at packing.
Is my car insured during a PCS shipment?
Yes. Every carrier holds an FMCSA license and cargo insurance. The signed condition form from pickup is your protection — verify the car against it at delivery before signing.
What if the car doesn't run?
We ship it with winch loading for about $150 to $250 more. Tell us at booking whether it rolls, steers, and brakes, and the right truck arrives the first time.
What's the cheapest time to PCS a car out of Enterprise?
Winter graduation cycles. Classes that finish between November and February ship into the year's lowest rates, while summer stacks the Army's peak season on top of everything.
Can incoming flight school families ship a car here?
Yes — the pipeline runs both directions. We pick up at your last posting, deliver during your arrival week, and stage at the Boll Weevil Circle until you have keys. Returning overseas families reunite with stateside-stored cars the same way.
Do civilian and contractor families get the same service?
The same carriers, staging, and rates — minus the military paperwork you don't need. Contractor relocations supporting the training fleet run on report dates just like PCS moves, and our invoices work with company reimbursement programs.
