Auto Transport in Mobile, Alabama

Alabama's port city — I-10 snowbird routes, Airbus relocations, and Gulf Coast shipping handled by carriers who know the Bayway.

Fully Insured (251) 230-3786

Mobile sits on the I-10 corridor between Florida and New Orleans, which makes it a natural artery for east-west car shipping. Port activity, the Airbus final assembly line at Brookley, and Austal's shipyard keep a steady flow of professional relocations, while snowbird season sends waves of vehicles between the Gulf Coast and the Midwest every fall and spring. New Orleans is just 145 miles west — one of the cheapest lanes on our board.

Local Know-How

Carrier Access in Mobile

Most pickups stage west of downtown — the Airport Boulevard and Schillinger Road corridors give big rigs room to work. Downtown and Midtown loads often meet near the Loop or a Government Boulevard lot. Heading east, carriers cross the Bayway on I-10; during summer storm season we watch weather closely since Bayway closures can shift pickup windows a day.

Local Questions

Mobile Auto Transport FAQs

Is Mobile a good starting point for shipping a car west?
One of the best in the South. I-10 carriers pass through Mobile daily heading to New Orleans, Houston, and beyond. The Mobile–New Orleans hop is about 145 miles and typically runs $300–$450 with next-day delivery.
Do you pick up in Tillmans Corner and Theodore?
Yes. South Mobile County along US-90 and I-10 is standard service area. Carriers usually meet at larger lots off Government Boulevard or Rangeline Road, where a full-size trailer can turn around safely.
How does hurricane season affect Mobile car shipping?
From June through November we track the Gulf closely. If a storm threatens, carriers may accelerate or delay pickup a day or two to stay ahead of Bayway and I-10 closures. Booking a flexible window during storm season keeps your price down.
