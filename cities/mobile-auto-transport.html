Mobile sits on the I-10 corridor between Florida and New Orleans, which makes it a natural artery for east-west car shipping. Port activity, the Airbus final assembly line at Brookley, and Austal's shipyard keep a steady flow of professional relocations, while snowbird season sends waves of vehicles between the Gulf Coast and the Midwest every fall and spring. New Orleans is just 145 miles west — one of the cheapest lanes on our board.

The port changes everything about Mobile shipping. Alabama's only deepwater harbor makes this the state's gateway for vehicle import and export — international relocations staging through the terminals, overseas-bound cars heading to processing, imports clearing toward new owners across the Southeast. Around the maritime core, Airbus's growing final assembly line recruits aerospace talent globally, Austal's shipyard cycles skilled trades, and the medical district's university hospitals draw specialists nationally. Mobile's shipping volume is coastal-city volume, with coastal-city variety.

Port City Shipping Knowledge

Mobile layers three calendars: the snowbird flood (October–December south, March–April north) that owns the Midwest lanes; the hurricane season (June–November) that occasionally shuffles pickup days around Gulf weather — always communicated ahead, never a surprise; and Mardi Gras, which is not a Louisiana import here but Mobile's own, closing downtown staging for the parade weeks each winter. We route around all three fluently. The clean windows — late January post-parades, early fall pre-snowbird — price at the floor.

Mobile's mix spans the spectrum: the working fleet rides open on Gulf-corridor economics, while the enclosed tier serves the port city's genuine collector community, the medical district's arriving specialists, and the export-bound vehicles whose overseas buyers expect delivery-condition perfection. Salt-air logic applies on the coastal approaches — enclosed spares a pristine finish the highway film — and hurricane-season loads near the water get weather-window planning as standard practice, not premium service.

Mobile relocations run every register: Airbus recruits arriving from Toulouse and Seattle and Wichita on program timelines, medical specialists following university start dates, maritime trades rotating with shipyard contracts, and the family currents of a metro region. A Mobile week moves the Gulf's full inventory: commuter fleets and family SUVs, shipyard trucks, the medical district's arriving imports, snowbird sedans by the dozen in season, and the port's international traffic — European purchases clearing customs, overseas-relocation vehicles heading to processing, the occasional gray-market rarity with paperwork thicker than its owner's manual. The staging solutions are generations established — the Loop and Government corridors for midtown, Airport Boulevard's western reaches for the sprawl, Rangeline for the south. Local Questions

Mobile Auto Transport FAQs

Is Mobile a good starting point for shipping a car west?
One of the best in the South. I-10 carriers pass through Mobile daily heading to New Orleans, Houston, and beyond. The Mobile–New Orleans hop is about 145 miles and typically runs $300–$450 with next-day delivery.
Do you pick up in Tillmans Corner and Theodore?
Yes. South Mobile County along US-90 and I-10 is standard service area. Carriers usually meet at larger lots off Government Boulevard or Rangeline Road, where a full-size trailer can turn around safely.
How does hurricane season affect Mobile car shipping?
From June through November we track the Gulf closely. If a storm threatens, carriers may accelerate or delay pickup a day or two to stay ahead of Bayway and I-10 closures. Booking a flexible window during storm season keeps your price down.
Can you deliver my car to the port for overseas shipping?
Yes — export deliveries to the processing centers run with condition documented to international standards: the photographic record and Bill of Lading that overseas buyers and marine insurers expect. You or your freight forwarder handle the ocean booking; we handle everything to the terminal gate.
I imported a car through the Port of Mobile — can you get it to me?
That's our other port specialty. Once customs clears, we receive at the terminal with the release paperwork handled, and deliver anywhere in the Southeast. First-time importers get walked through the document sequence before the ship docks.
How does hurricane season affect my Mobile shipment?
From June through November we watch the Gulf and occasionally shuffle pickup days ahead of weather — always communicated in advance, never a surprise charge. Flexible booking windows in storm season keep prices down and options open.
What happens to shipping during Mardi Gras?
Downtown staging closes for the parade weeks — Mobile's carnival is real infrastructure — so winter shipments route to the midtown and western corridors. Book around the parade calendar and the season costs you nothing but beads.
What does Mobile to Houston cost on the I-10 corridor?
Typically $600–$825 with the Gulf corridor's constant traffic keeping it liquid — 470 miles of the busiest east-west lane in the South. Flexible dates catch through-carriers filling slots daily.
Do Airbus relocations ship through you?
Regularly — aerospace arrivals from Europe and across the U.S. land on program timelines with corporate documentation handled to their administrators' formats. Brookley-orbit delivery and cross-bay staging cover wherever the household settles.
