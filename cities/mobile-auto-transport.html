Auto Transport in Mobile, Alabama

Ship your car to or from Mobile with carriers that run I-10 and I-65 every day. Snowbirds, port shipments, and family moves are our specialty.

Fully Insured (251) 230-3786

Mobile is a great city to ship a car from. Two big highways meet here: I-10 runs east and west along the Gulf Coast, and I-65 runs north through the rest of Alabama. Car carriers use both roads every single day. That keeps prices fair and pickups quick. Our Mobile customers include families moving for work, retirees heading south for the winter, workers at Airbus and the shipyards, and people who bought or sold a car in another state.

Shipping a car with Heartland Auto Transport is simpler than most people think. Start with a free quote from the form above, or just call. We book a licensed, insured carrier for your route. The driver meets you, inspects the car with you, and writes everything down on a form called a Bill of Lading. Your car rides on the truck, and you get updates along the way. At delivery, you compare the car to that form, sign, and you're finished.

Local Know-How

How Car Pickup Works in Mobile

Car carriers are long trucks, so drivers pick meeting spots with room to load. In Mobile, that usually means big parking lots along Airport Boulevard, Schillinger Road, or Government Boulevard. Downtown streets are too tight for the truck, so downtown customers meet a few blocks away at a bigger lot. The driver calls about an hour before pickup to set the exact spot. Loading takes about 20 minutes.

These prices are for open transport, the standard way to ship. Your car rides on an open trailer with several other cars. It's safe, cheap, and fast. The New Orleans run often delivers the same day. Houston and Tampa take 2 to 3 days. If your car is a classic or a luxury model, enclosed transport puts it inside a covered trailer for 60 to 80 percent more.

What changes the price? Three main things. First, size: trucks and big SUVs cost $75 to $150 more than sedans. Second, season: winter snowbird season (October to December) raises prices on routes to the Midwest, and summer raises prices everywhere. Third, condition: a car that won't start costs $150 to $250 extra because the driver has to winch it onto the trailer. Tell us all of this up front and your quote will be accurate.

Local Insight

Tips for Shipping a Car in Mobile

Snowbird season is our busiest time. From October through December, cars pour into the Gulf Coast from Ohio, Michigan, Indiana, and Illinois. From mid-March through April, they all go back north. If you're a snowbird, book about two weeks ahead during these months. Booking the round trip at once costs less than two separate orders.

Hurricane season runs June through November. If a storm heads toward the Gulf, your pickup might move a day earlier or later to stay safe. We always call you first — there are no surprise changes and no extra charges for weather delays.

Buying or selling a car through the Port of Mobile? We can help. For cars going overseas, we deliver to the port processing center with photos and paperwork done right. For cars arriving from overseas, we pick up at the terminal after customs clears them and bring the car to your door.

Working at Airbus or the shipyard? We move employee cars all the time. We can deliver your car during your first week at the new job and meet you near Brookley or your apartment if your housing isn't ready yet.

Live across the bay in Daphne or Fairhope? No problem. We cover both sides of Mobile Bay at the same fair rates. Just tell us which side you're on and we'll set the meeting spot there.

One local tip: summer afternoon storms come fast on the coast. Morning pickups are the smoothest from June through September. Your driver will usually suggest a morning window anyway — take it.

Shipping a golf cart to a bay-area neighborhood? We do it all the time. Carts cost much less than cars to ship and ride strapped on the same trucks. Gated communities just need a heads-up so the driver can get through the gate.

Winter and early fall are the cheapest times to ship from Mobile — except on snowbird routes, where fall is the busy season. If your route heads north to the Midwest, spring and fall cost more. Everywhere else, January and February are the bargain months.

Local Questions

Mobile Auto Transport FAQs

How much does it cost to ship a car from Mobile to New Orleans?
About $300 to $450 for a regular car. It's only 145 miles down I-10, and carriers run that road all day. Many New Orleans shipments are picked up and delivered on the same day.
When should snowbirds book their car shipment?
About two weeks ahead during the busy months — October through December going south, and mid-March through April going north. Booking your round trip at the same time saves money over two separate bookings.
What happens if a hurricane comes during my shipment?
We watch Gulf weather closely from June through November. If a storm threatens, we may move your pickup a day earlier or later to keep your car safe. We always call you first, and weather changes never cost extra.
Can you deliver my car to the Port of Mobile for overseas shipping?
Yes. We take your car to the port processing center with full photos and the paperwork done to shipping standards. You or your shipping agent handle the boat part — we handle everything up to the port gate.
Can you pick up a car I imported through the port?
Yes. After customs clears your car, we pick it up at the terminal and deliver it to your door — anywhere in the Southeast. If it's your first import, we'll walk you through the paperwork before the ship arrives.
Do you cover Daphne and Fairhope too?
Yes, both sides of the bay ship at the same rates. Eastern Shore customers usually meet the driver near Jubilee Square in Daphne or along Greeno Road in Fairhope.
Is my car insured on the truck?
Yes. Every carrier is FMCSA-licensed and insured. The driver records your car's condition at pickup, and you check it at delivery. Any transit damage is covered by the carrier's insurance.
How do I prepare my car for shipping?
Remove personal items, leave about a quarter tank of gas, turn off alarms and toll tags, and give the car a quick wash so its condition is easy to see and photograph. Bring your keys to the pickup.
What if my car doesn't run?
We can still ship it. The driver brings a winch to pull it onto the trailer, which adds about $150 to $250. Just tell us when you book so the right truck shows up.
Can you ship a golf cart to Mobile or the Eastern Shore?
Yes. Golf carts cost much less than cars and ride strapped on the same open trucks. For fancy carts, enclosed shipping is available too. We deliver to bay-area neighborhoods weekly.
Do you ship cars for Airbus and shipyard workers?
Yes, all the time. We can deliver your car during your first week at the new job and meet you near Brookley or your apartment if your housing isn't set yet. Company moving programs can be billed directly when allowed.
How long does shipping from Mobile to Houston take?
Usually 2 to 3 days. It's about 470 miles on I-10, a road carriers drive every day. Flexible pickup dates get you the best price on this route.
