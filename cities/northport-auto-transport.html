Local Questions

Northport Auto Transport FAQs

Is Northport priced the same as Tuscaloosa?
Yes — carriers treat the river cities as one market. Staging on the Northport side can actually speed things up on football weekends, when campus-side loading gets impossible.
Where do Highway 69 North subdivisions meet carriers?
The retail centers along 69 North near the Mitt Lary corridor. It keeps drivers off the subdivision streets and puts the meetup within a few minutes of the newer neighborhoods.
Do football Saturdays affect Northport pickups?
Less than Tuscaloosa proper, but we still schedule around the seven home games — bridge traffic backs up river-wide. Flexible dates around game weekends often draw better bids.
Can I use Northport staging to dodge game-day gridlock?
Yes — it's the metro's best scheduling trick. Home-Saturday bridge traffic locks campus-side loading while Northport's corridors stay workable, so fall shipments that can meet north-bank keep weekends available. Tell dispatch at booking and the routing plans for it.
How does Kentuck Festival week affect shipping?
Downtown Northport fills for the October weekend, so that week's staging shifts up McFarland — a two-minute adjustment we make automatically. Artist and festival-vehicle shipments around the event book a couple weeks ahead; the arts calendar concentrates them.
Do you coordinate cross-river moves as one booking?
Yes — a Northport pickup with a Tuscaloosa delivery, or an estate spanning both banks, sequences as a single project on one route. The metro ships as one market; your booking should too.
What does Northport to Memphis cost?
Typically $375–$525 on the western corridor — and west-approaching carriers often hit the north bank first, which can advance Memphis-lane pickups a day when routing aligns.
Is the Highway 69 North growth corridor fully covered?

Can you ship a bass boat tow rig seasonally?

Do medical professionals relocating to the McFarland corridor get start-date timing?
Yes — hospital relocations deliver against your credentialing and start date, with staging on the north-bank corridor minutes from the medical district. If onboarding shifts, the delivery shifts with it at no charge.
