Three things set your price. Size: trucks and big SUVs cost $75 to $150 more than sedans. Season: August move-in and May move-out raise metro prices 15 to 25 percent, and January drops them to the floor. Condition: a car that won't start adds $150 to $250 for winch loading. One Northport-only lever: telling us you can meet on either side of the river. Carriers group their stops by bank, and that flexibility often speeds up your pickup.

Tips for Shipping a Car in Northport

Use the game-day trick. On Alabama's seven home Saturdays, bridge traffic locks campus-side loading — but Northport's corridors stay workable. Fall shippers who can meet on the north bank keep weekends available that vanish from the campus-side calendar. Tell us at booking and the routing plans for it.

Kentuck Festival fills downtown Northport for a weekend each October. That week's pickups shift up McFarland automatically at no charge. Artists shipping work vehicles for the festival should book a couple weeks out — the arts calendar bunches them together.

Medical workers joining the McFarland corridor get start-date timing. We deliver during your first week, stage minutes from the hospital if your housing isn't set, and shift the schedule free if your credentialing date moves.

Cross-river households book once, not twice. A Northport pickup with a Tuscaloosa delivery — or an estate spanning both banks — runs as one booking on one route.

Student cars flow through both banks each August and May. We text the student the time and spot directly, and the north-bank meetups skip the campus crowds entirely.

The Highway 69 North subdivisions ship at metro rates, with newer entrances confirmed at booking. Coker and the western county meet at the Highway 82 junctions — and the western approach actually favors them, since Memphis-route carriers pass there first.

Bass-boat season? The tow rig ships like any vehicle, timed ahead of your first weekend on the water. Seasonal round trips booked together cost less than two singles.

January and February are the metro's cheap months. Between semesters and before spring, carriers have space and price it to move. Flexible dates catch the bottom.

Local Questions

Northport Auto Transport FAQs

Is Northport really the same price as Tuscaloosa?
Yes — one metro, one rate table. The route sets the price, not which bank you live on. What Northport adds is scheduling: west-coming carriers reach the north bank first, and game-day weekends stay workable here.
How does the game-day trick work?
Home football Saturdays lock campus-side loading, but Northport's staging corridors stay open. If your fall shipment can meet on the north bank, you keep weekends available that campus-side shippers lose. Just tell us at booking.
What happens during Kentuck Festival week?
Downtown fills for the October weekend, so pickups shift up McFarland — a two-minute change we make automatically. Festival-bound vehicles should book a couple weeks ahead.
Can one booking cover both sides of the river?
Yes. A Northport pickup with a Tuscaloosa delivery, or a family project spanning both banks, runs as a single booking on one route. The metro ships as one market and your paperwork should too.
What does Northport to Memphis cost?
About $375 to $525, usually a day or two in transit. Memphis-route carriers pass the north bank first, which sometimes moves your pickup up a day when routing aligns.
Do medical workers get start-date delivery?
Yes. We deliver during your first week on the McFarland corridor, stage near the hospital if housing lags, and move the schedule free if your credentialing date shifts.
Is Coker and the western county covered?
Yes, at metro rates. Western addresses meet at the Highway 82 junctions, and the west-first carrier approach often works in their favor on pickup timing.
Can you ship my student's car through the north bank?
Yes — and in the busy weeks it's often smoother than campus-side. We text the student directly with the time and place, and the meetup skips the campus crowds.
Is my car insured while it ships?
Yes. Every carrier holds an FMCSA license and cargo insurance. The signed condition report from pickup is your record — check the car against it at delivery.
What if the car doesn't run?
Winch loading covers it for $150 to $250 more. Tell us at booking whether it rolls, steers, and brakes, and the right truck arrives.
How do I prepare the car?
Take out personal items, leave about a quarter tank, turn off the alarm and toll tag, and rinse it clean for honest photos. Bring keys and ID.
When is shipping cheapest here?
January and February, between semesters. Carriers have open space and drop prices to fill it. August and May cost the most — book three weeks out if you must ship then.
Can you ship a classic or an artist's showpiece vehicle?
Yes — the Kentuck orbit means Northport ships more interesting vehicles than towns its size usually do. Restored classics and rolling artwork ride enclosed with soft straps and full photos before loading, and event deliveries time to the festival calendar.
How fast are Northport pickups?
Usually 1 to 3 business days, matching the metro. The north bank's west-first carrier approach sometimes shaves a day when routing aligns, and game weekends never slow this side of the river the way they slow campus.
Can two families share one carrier from the north bank?
When destinations and weeks match, yes — each saves 10 to 20 percent per vehicle. August student cohorts and June family moves create matches regularly, so tell dispatch your timing and destination.
Can you deliver a dealer-bought car to Northport?
Yes. We coordinate with the dealer's delivery desk in Birmingham, Memphis, or wherever you bought, pick up once the release paperwork clears, and land the car at your driveway or the McFarland meetup — usually within two days for regional purchases, sticker still on the glass.
