Local Questions

Huntsville Auto Transport FAQs

Can you handle a PCS move from Redstone Arsenal?
Yes — military moves are a large share of our Huntsville business. Carriers can't drive onto the Arsenal, so we arrange pickup just outside the gates, usually near Gate 9 off I-565. We schedule around your report date and can hold delivery timing to match your arrival at the new duty station.
How much is car shipping from Huntsville to Nashville?
It's one of the shortest interstate lanes we run — about 115 miles straight up I-65. Expect roughly $300–$450 for a standard vehicle on an open carrier, often with next-day delivery.
Do carriers serve Harvest, Monrovia, and Meridianville?
Yes. The fast-growing communities north and west of Huntsville are all standard service area. Drivers typically use US-72 or Highway 53 and meet at a nearby shopping center if a subdivision's streets are too tight for the trailer.
