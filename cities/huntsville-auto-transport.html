Local Know-How

How Car Pickup Works in Huntsville

Big car-hauling trucks are about 75 feet long, so they can't fit on every street. In Huntsville, drivers usually meet customers at large parking lots near Bridge Street, MidCity, or along Memorial Parkway. If you live on Redstone Arsenal, the driver cannot come through the gate. Instead, you meet just outside Gate 9 near I-565. It takes about 15 minutes and the driver will call you to set the exact spot.

The prices above are for open transport, which is the normal way to ship a car. Your car rides on an open truck with 7 to 9 other cars, the same way new cars get to dealerships. Short trips like Nashville often deliver the next day. Longer trips like Denver take about a week. If you want extra protection, enclosed transport puts your car inside a covered trailer, but it costs 60 to 80 percent more.

A few things change the price. Bigger vehicles cost more. A pickup truck or large SUV usually costs $75 to $150 more than a sedan because it takes up more room on the trailer. Summer costs more than winter because more people move in summer. And a car that doesn't run costs about $150 to $250 extra, because the driver needs a winch to pull it onto the truck. Tell us these things when you get your quote so the price you see is the price you pay.

Local Insight

Tips for Shipping a Car in Huntsville

Book early if you can. When Redstone, the FBI, or a defense company hires a new group of people, lots of cars ship to Huntsville at the same time. Booking a week or two ahead gets you a better price and your choice of dates. If you're flexible on your pickup day, tell us. Flexible dates almost always cost less.

Military move? We do these every week. Carriers can't drive onto the Arsenal, so we set up a meeting spot just outside the gate. We time the pickup to your orders. If the Army or Air Force changes your report date, we change your pickup date for free.

Moving to Huntsville for a new job? Most families do it this way: drive one car to town, and ship the second car so it arrives the same week. If your house isn't ready yet, we can meet you at a parking lot near your hotel or apartment. The car doesn't need an address, just you and your keys.

Electric cars are welcome. Huntsville ships more EVs than any other city in Alabama. Teslas, Rivians, and Lightnings all ride on normal carriers. We just ask that your battery is charged between 30 and 80 percent at pickup, and we tell the driver how to put your car in transport mode.

Shipping a car for a college student? We move cars to and from campuses all over the country each August and May. We text your student directly to set up the handoff, so you don't have to pass messages back and forth.

One local tip: avoid scheduling your pickup at 7 a.m. or 5 p.m. Gate 9 traffic and Research Park traffic slow everything down at those times. A mid-morning pickup usually takes 20 minutes start to finish.

Selling a car to a buyer in another state? Shipping protects both of you. The driver's condition report shows the buyer exactly what they're getting, and the insurance covers the trip. Many Huntsville sellers offer delivered pricing to reach more buyers.

Winter is the cheapest time to ship. From January through February, fewer people move, so carriers drop their prices. If your dates are flexible, a winter shipment can save you $50 to $100 compared to summer.

Local Questions

Huntsville Auto Transport FAQs

How fast can you pick up my car in Huntsville?
Usually in 1 to 2 business days. Huntsville sits on busy trucking routes, so carriers are almost always close by. During the summer moving rush it might take 3 days. Booking a week ahead locks in your date.
How much does it cost to ship a car from Huntsville to Nashville?
About $300 to $450 for a normal car on an open carrier. It's only 115 miles, so the car often gets picked up and delivered the same day or the next day. It's one of the cheapest routes in the country.
Can you pick up my car on Redstone Arsenal?
Not on the base itself — carriers can't go through the gate. We meet you just outside Gate 9 near I-565 instead. It's quick and easy, and we do it every week for military and civilian workers.
Do you ship electric cars like Teslas?
Yes, all the time. EVs ride on the same trucks as gas cars. We ask for a battery charge between 30 and 80 percent and we make sure the driver knows how to use your car's transport mode. That's the only difference.
What if my military orders change?
We move your pickup date for free. Orders change all the time, and our dispatchers are used to it. Just call or text us the new date and we'll adjust the schedule.
How do I get my car ready for shipping?
Take out your personal stuff, leave about a quarter tank of gas, turn off toll tags and alarms, and wash the car so the driver can see its condition clearly. Have your keys ready. That's all it takes.
Is my car insured during the trip?
Yes. Every carrier we use is licensed by the FMCSA and carries cargo insurance. The driver writes down your car's condition at pickup, and you check it again at delivery. If anything happened in between, the insurance covers it.
Can my college student's car ship to their school?
Yes. We ship student cars every August and May. We coordinate directly with your student by text so the handoff is easy. Book 3 to 4 weeks before move-in week for the best price.
Should I ship my car open or enclosed?
Open is right for almost everyone. It's how 9 out of 10 cars ship, including brand-new cars going to dealers. Choose enclosed if you have a classic, luxury, or show car that needs full protection. Enclosed costs 60 to 80 percent more.
Can you ship a car that doesn't run?
Yes. Tell us when you book, and we'll send a truck with a winch to pull the car onto the trailer. It adds about $150 to $250. If the car doesn't roll or steer at all, we may need a flatbed instead — we'll sort that out in the quote.
Do you ship to Madison, Athens, and Decatur too?
Yes. The whole Huntsville metro area — Madison, Athens, Decatur, Harvest, Meridianville — ships at the same rates. Carriers run the same roads no matter which side of town you're on.
How do I pay for car shipping?
Most customers pay part when a carrier is assigned and the rest at delivery. The delivery payment usually goes to the driver by cash or certified funds. We explain the exact terms on your order confirmation before you commit.
