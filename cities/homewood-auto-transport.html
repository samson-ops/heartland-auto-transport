Local Questions

Homewood Auto Transport FAQs

Why can't the truck load on my Edgewood street?
Street width and tree canopy. A loaded auto carrier needs clearance and turning room that Homewood's older blocks weren't designed for. The Wildwood lot is five minutes away and makes loading faster and safer for your vehicle.
Do you handle Samford student vehicles?
Every semester. August and May are the peak windows, and Lakeshore Drive staging puts the meeting point practically at the campus edge. Book two to three weeks ahead of move-in week for the best rates.
Is Homewood priced differently than Birmingham?
No — it's the same metro market. Your rate is set by the lane and vehicle, not the suburb.
I matched to a residency across the country — how should I plan the car?
Book when you match, not when you graduate. June's residency lanes fill with your classmates, and early bookings catch shared-carrier pricing on common destinations like Denver and the Carolinas. We deliver against your orientation date and can stage near the hospital if housing isn't settled.
Can two matched classmates share a carrier?
If you're heading to the same metro the same week, yes — and you'll each save 10–20%. Match lists make this surprisingly common. Tell us your destination and dates early and dispatch will look for classmates on the lane.
How do Samford student shipments work?
August and May, staged along Lakeshore minutes from campus. We coordinate with move-in weekends and communicate directly with the student for the handoff. Graduation-season pickups book out by mid-April; the diploma-to-departure window is tighter than families expect.
Where exactly is the Wildwood meetup?
The Wildwood shopping center off Lakeshore — flat, spacious, and seconds from I-65. The driver confirms the exact corner when scheduling. From most Homewood addresses it's a five-minute drive, and it's why the neighborhood's tightest streets never slow its pickups.
What does Homewood to Denver cost for a residency move?
Typically $1,000–$1,300 for standard vehicles, with June's synchronized medical migration occasionally unlocking shared-carrier pricing below that. Seven to ten days transit; we time delivery to orientation.
Is Homewood priced differently than Birmingham?
No — same metro market, same lane rates. What Homewood adds is staging efficiency: the Wildwood-Lakeshore pattern is so established that pickups here often dispatch faster than downtown's.
Can you consolidate cars from my parents' garages in other states?
It's a classic first-house Homewood request — the sedan that stayed in Ohio, the truck at the family place in Tennessee, finally reunited in an Edgewood driveway. We sequence pickups from each origin and land the deliveries within the same week, staged at Wildwood so the narrow blocks never enter the equation.
