Superspeedway country — race-week logistics, classic-car experience, and I-20 lane access via Eastaboga.

Talladega shipping has a rhythm no other Alabama market has: two race weeks a year when 175,000 people descend on the Superspeedway and every carrier in the region reroutes. We plan around it — and take advantage of it, since race traffic brings extra trucks to the area. The rest of the year, Talladega is quiet I-20-corridor shipping with strong classic-car volume headed to and from collectors and the racing community.

Local Know-How

Talladega Pickup Logistics

Most loads stage at the Highway 77/I-20 interchange retail corners near Eastaboga or at the McMillan Boulevard centers in town. During race weeks (spring and fall), we schedule pickups for early morning or shift them a day to dodge track traffic on Speedway Boulevard. Sylacauga-side moves meet along US-280.

  • Race-week aware scheduling around both annual NASCAR weekends
  • Classic and race-car shipping experience, open or enclosed
  • I-20 access via Highway 77 keeps standard lanes fast and affordable

Local Questions

Talladega Auto Transport FAQs

Can you ship a car during Talladega race week?
Yes, with planning. We schedule early-morning pickups or shift a day to avoid Speedway Boulevard congestion. Interestingly, race weeks bring extra car haulers to the area, so flexible dates can actually catch a good rate.
Do you transport race cars or classics from Talladega?
Frequently — it comes with the territory. Enclosed transport is the usual choice for competition and collector vehicles, and we work with carriers experienced in low-clearance loading.
What's the closest big lane from Talladega?
Birmingham (55 miles) and Atlanta (115 miles) both run daily via I-20 at Eastaboga. Short hops like these typically price between $275–$425.
