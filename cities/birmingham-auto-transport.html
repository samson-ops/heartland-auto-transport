Auto Transport in Birmingham, Alabama

Birmingham is the best-connected city in Alabama for car shipping. Four interstates meet here: I-20, I-59, I-65, and I-22, plus the I-459 loop around the city. Car carriers cross Birmingham every day heading to Atlanta, Nashville, Dallas, Florida, and beyond. All those trucks compete for your business, which keeps prices down and pickup times short. Most Birmingham pickups happen within 1 to 2 business days.

The Heartland Auto Transport process is simple. Get your free quote with the form above or give us a call. We assign a licensed, insured carrier to your route. On pickup day, the driver meets you, walks around the car with you, and writes down its condition. Your car rides safely on the truck while you get updates. At delivery you check the car, sign the form, and you're done. Clear pricing, no hidden fees, and real people who answer the phone.

Local Know-How

How Car Pickup Works in Birmingham

Car carriers are about 75 feet long, so downtown streets don't work for loading. Drivers usually meet customers at big lots near Uptown, the Summit, or along the US-280 corridor. In the suburbs, most driveways work fine for door-to-door pickup. If you live over the mountain in Homewood, Vestavia, or Mountain Brook, the driver will suggest a lot on Highway 31 — the mountain streets are too steep and narrow for a loaded truck.

These prices are for open transport — the standard, safe way most cars ship. Your car rides on an open trailer with other vehicles, just like new cars going to a dealership. Atlanta and Nashville often deliver the next day. Dallas and Chicago take 3 to 5 days. Enclosed transport, where your car rides inside a covered trailer, is available for classics and luxury cars at 60 to 80 percent more.

Here's what changes your price. Vehicle size matters: pickups and big SUVs run $75 to $150 more than sedans. The season matters: summer is the busiest and most expensive time, while January and February are the cheapest. A car that doesn't run adds $150 to $250 for winch loading. And flexible dates save money — if you can hand off the car any day that week, carriers will often bid lower to fill an open spot.

Local Insight

Tips for Shipping a Car in Birmingham

Moving for a job or a residency at UAB? We ship cars for doctors, nurses, and hospital staff every June and July. We can deliver your car during your first week and meet you near the hospital if you're still looking for an apartment. If you matched to a program in another city, book when you match — your classmates are shipping the same routes at the same time.

Booking early always helps. Two weeks ahead gets you the best mix of price and choice. But because so many trucks pass through Birmingham, we can often handle last-minute moves too. If your plans change suddenly, call anyway — same-week pickup here is normal, not a miracle.

Shipping two or more cars? Put them on the same truck. Families moving with two cars save 10 to 20 percent per car when both ride together. One pickup, one delivery, one bill.

Buying a car from an auction or dealer in another city? We bring cars into Birmingham every week from Atlanta, Nashville, and Dallas auctions. Give us your buyer number and the lot info, and we'll pick it up before storage fees start.

Have a classic or collector car? Birmingham has one of the biggest car-collector communities in the South. We ship show cars and classics in enclosed trailers with photos taken before loading. Ask for an enclosed quote and we'll price it straight.

One local tip: if you live downtown or in a loft, don't worry about the truck reaching your building. Meeting at Uptown or a 20th Street lot takes five minutes and makes loading faster and safer for your car.

Estate sales and family transfers are common here. If you're sending a loved one's car to a family member in another state, we document the car's condition carefully at pickup and help the person on the other end through their first delivery. We're patient with these — they matter.

Need a specialty truck? Birmingham has the state's best access to enclosed trailers, liftgate rigs for low cars, and winch trucks for cars that don't run. Whatever your vehicle needs, the right equipment is usually only hours away, not days.

Local Questions

Birmingham Auto Transport FAQs

How fast can you pick up a car in Birmingham?
Usually within 1 to 2 business days — the fastest in Alabama. Four interstates bring carriers through the city constantly, so there's almost always a truck nearby with an open spot.
How much is it to ship a car from Birmingham to Atlanta?
About $350 to $500 for a standard car. It's 150 miles down I-20, one of the busiest car-hauling routes in the South. Next-day delivery is normal.
Can you ship cars for UAB students, residents, and staff?
Yes, every summer. We deliver during your first week in town and can meet near the hospital if you're still apartment hunting. If you matched to a residency in another city, book when you match for the best price.
Do two cars on the same truck cost less?
Yes — about 10 to 20 percent less per car. Families moving with two vehicles should always ship them together. One pickup, one delivery, one lower bill.
Can you pick up a car I won at an auction?
Yes. We pick up from Copart, IAA, Manheim, and dealer auctions every week. Send us your buyer number, the lot number, and the location, and we'll get the car out before storage fees pile up.
Where does the truck meet me if I live downtown?
At a big lot nearby — usually Uptown or along 20th Street. Downtown blocks are too tight for a 75-foot truck. The meeting spot is a five-minute drive and makes loading quicker and safer.
Is Hoover or Homewood more expensive than Birmingham?
No. The whole metro ships at the same price. Your cost depends on the route and your vehicle, not which suburb you live in.
What's the cheapest time to ship from Birmingham?
January and February. Fewer people move in winter, so carriers lower their prices. Summer is the most expensive season because everyone moves at once.
Is my car insured during transport?
Yes. Every carrier we use is licensed and insured. The driver documents your car's condition at pickup, you verify it at delivery, and the carrier's insurance covers anything that happens in between.
Can you ship a car from an estate to a family member?
Yes, and we handle these with care. We photograph the car fully at pickup, work with the executor on timing, and walk the family member through delivery if they've never received a shipped car before.
What does Birmingham to Nashville cost?
About $350 to $525 for a standard car. It's 190 miles straight up I-65, and carriers run it daily in both directions. Next-day delivery is common.
Can you ship motorcycles from Birmingham?
Yes. Motorcycles ride strapped and wheel-chocked on open carriers, or crated in enclosed trailers for expensive bikes. They cost less than cars because they take up less room on the truck.
Can I ship my car and fly to my new city?
That's what most people do. Ship the car a few days before your flight, fly to your new city, and the car arrives right behind you. If it gets there before you do, the driver can hold delivery a day or meet you at a lot near your hotel. You travel light and skip a long drive.
