Local Insight

Tips for Shipping a Car in Anniston

Depot workers taking a federal transfer: keep your invoice and the signed condition form. Those are the two documents the government's moving reimbursement system asks for, and ours are formatted for it. If your report date shifts, your pickup shifts with it at no charge.

Sending a project car to a body shop or buying one from out of state? Tell us honestly what it can do — roll, steer, brake, or none of the above. Winch loading covers most projects, a rollback covers the rest, and honesty at booking is what keeps the schedule intact.

Motorcycles ship all the time here. Bikes ride strapped to wheel chocks on open carriers, or crated inside enclosed trailers for the valuable ones. Either way they cost well under car rates because they use a fraction of the space.

Jacksonville State families: student cars ship each August and May, staged along the Highway 21 corridor minutes from campus. We text the student the time and place so the handoff happens without you relaying messages.

The McClellan neighborhoods load beautifully — those wide former-base streets were built for convoys. Drivers stage along the main boulevard for addresses tucked into the newer sections.

Live toward Choccolocco or the mountain communities? Meet the driver at the Highway 21 or Iron Mountain junctions. It's a ten-minute drive for you and it saves the driver an hour of three-point turns with ten cars on deck.

Tuesday through Thursday pickups are the sweet spot. I-20 carrier traffic peaks midweek as drivers position between Atlanta and the Dallas end of the corridor — more trucks bidding means tighter windows and better prices.

Shipping a restored classic to a show or a buyer? Enclosed transport with soft straps and full photos before loading is the standard. The 90-mile Atlanta run makes weekend shows realistic, and Charlotte's collector market is a steady route from here.

Local Questions

Anniston Auto Transport FAQs

How do federal Depot transfers work with you?
We time pickup to your report date and provide the itemized invoice and signed condition form the federal reimbursement system requires. Date changes move your pickup free — Depot schedules shift, and we plan for it.
Can you ship a project car that doesn't run?
Yes — it's routine Anniston work. A car that rolls gets winch loading for $150 to $250 extra. A car that won't roll needs a rollback, which we arrange up front. Just tell us honestly what it can do.
How much is Anniston to Atlanta?
About $275 to $425 for a standard car. At 90 miles it's one of the shortest interstate runs we offer, and eastbound carriers repositioning toward Atlanta often bid it low. Same-day delivery happens regularly.
Do you ship motorcycles?
All the time. Bikes ride wheel-chocked and strapped on open carriers, or crated in enclosed trailers for high-value machines. Pricing runs well below car rates.
How does the Oxford Exchange meetup work?
The driver calls or texts about an hour out. You drive to the outer lots at exit 188, the walk-around inspection and loading take about twenty minutes, and you leave with your signed copy of the condition form and the driver's number.
Is Jacksonville covered at Anniston prices?
Yes — the whole county ships as one market. JSU student shipments stage along Highway 21 each semester, timed to the university's move-in calendar.
What's the best day of the week to schedule a pickup?
Midweek, Tuesday through Thursday. That's when I-20 carrier traffic peaks and the most trucks bid on Anniston loads, which means tighter windows and better prices than Mondays or weekends.
Can you ship a restored classic to a car show?
Yes, and we do it with the care the work deserves — enclosed trailer, soft straps, and complete photos before loading. Atlanta shows are 90 minutes away, and the Southeast circuit is a standing route.
Is my car insured during the trip?
Yes. Every carrier holds an FMCSA license and cargo insurance. The condition form you sign at pickup is your protection — match the car to it at delivery before you sign off.
How should I prepare the car?
Take your things out, leave about a quarter tank of fuel, disarm the alarm, and clean it enough that the photos show its true condition. Bring keys and ID to the meetup.
What does Anniston to Charlotte cost?
About $450 to $650, with 2 to 3 day delivery. The Carolina corridor runs steadily out of the I-20 junction, and collector-car traffic keeps it warm year-round.
When is shipping cheapest from Anniston?
Late January and February. Winter trailer space gets discounted to fill, and flexible dates catch the bottom of every range. Summer costs the most, like everywhere.
Can you pick up from the McClellan district?
Yes — those wide former-base streets load beautifully, and drivers stage along the main boulevard for the newer clusters. McClellan ships at the same county rate as everywhere else in Anniston.
What does paperwork look like on pickup day?
Just keys and someone 18 or older to sign the condition form — you don't need the title to ship a car you own. If a friend or shop is releasing the vehicle for you, give us their name in advance and the driver will expect them.
