Local Questions

Anniston Auto Transport FAQs

Do you handle Anniston Army Depot relocations?
Regularly. Depot civilian transfers follow federal moving timelines, and we schedule pickup at the Bynum-area staging corners to match. If your report date moves, we adjust without penalty.
What's the Anniston-to-Atlanta rate?
At roughly 90 miles it's one of the shortest interstate lanes we run — typically $300–$425 for a standard vehicle, often delivered the same day it's picked up.
Where do Oxford pickups happen?
The Oxford Exchange at exit 188. It's the carrier favorite for the whole county — straight off I-20 with plenty of loading room.
