Local Questions

Foley Auto Transport FAQs

How does the snowbird round trip work from Foley?
Book the fall delivery and spring return together — the pair prices below two singles, and repeat clients hold the same windows every year. Southbound peaks October through November, northbound mid-March through April, and two weeks' notice in season keeps you choosing carriers.
Why meet in Foley instead of my Gulf Shores address?
Bridge traffic, roundabout geometry, and summer storms — island loading fights a 75-foot trailer on every front. The Tanger meetup costs fifteen minutes and reliably saves half a day. Off-season, ask and we can sometimes work closer.
Can I ship my car down for a summer job at the beach?
Yes — it's a Foley specialty. Seasonal hospitality round trips ship down in April or May and home in September, priced as a pair, with Tanger staging if your summer housing isn't set on arrival.
Do you really ship that many golf carts?
South Baldwin might lead the state. Carts ride strapped on open decks or enclosed for the showpieces, priced well under car rates, flowing between Midwest garages and beach neighborhoods on the seasonal clock.
What does the Columbus, Ohio route cost from Foley?
About $750 to $975 for a standard car, roughly four days door to door. It firms toward the top in snowbird season and softens to the floor in late winter — timing is most of the price on this one.
What happens if a storm comes up during my shipment?
From June through November we watch the Gulf and may shift your pickup a day to stay ahead of weather. We call first, every time, and weather moves never carry a charge.
Is Magnolia Springs' canopy a problem for pickup?
For the trailer, yes — the oaks over the river roads don't clear a loaded deck. Those addresses stage at the Highway 98 junctions minutes away, and the canopy stays exactly as it should.
Are Elberta and Summerdale covered at Foley prices?
Yes — the whole south county quotes as one market. Rural addresses meet at the wide-shouldered junctions, and the route sets the price wherever you are on the map.
Is my car insured on the trip?
Yes. Carriers hold FMCSA licenses and cargo insurance, and the signed condition record from pickup is your proof. Check the car against it at delivery before you sign.
What if my car won't start?
Winch loading covers it for about $150 to $250 extra. Tell us at booking whether it rolls, steers, and brakes, and the right truck shows up the first time.
How should I prepare the car for shipping?
Personal items out, roughly a quarter tank in, alarm and toll tag off, and a rinse so the condition photos read true. Keys and ID at the handoff complete the list.
When is the cheapest time to ship from Foley?
January, February, and September — the gaps between migrations. Flexible dates in those months catch the year's lowest prices on every route north.
Can you deliver a car before my beach house closes?
Yes. We stage at Tanger and hand off wherever you're staying — the car needs you and your keys, not a finished address. If the closing slips a day or two, the schedule flexes free.
Do you move contractor trucks for the building boom?
Weekly. Work trucks and equipment-hauling pickups ship at standard rates plus the usual size difference, and crews relocating for a project can put several vehicles on one carrier at a per-truck discount.
