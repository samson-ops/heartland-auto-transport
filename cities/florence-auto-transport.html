Local Insight

Tips for Shipping a Car in Florence

Don't reposition your car to the interstate. Florence customers ask about this constantly, so here's the straight answer: it saves zero dollars. Prices are set by the route from the Shoals, and carriers already group their stops here. Driving 45 miles to Athens just costs you a favor from a friend.

UNA families: we ship student cars every August and May. The campus streets can't take a car hauler, so students meet the driver at Cox Creek, ten minutes away. We text them the time and place directly, and you get confirmation when the handoff is done.

Give us side-of-the-river flexibility if you can. Carriers run Florence stops and Muscle Shoals-side stops in separate passes. A customer who can meet on either side often gets picked up a full day sooner.

TVA and river-industry moves run on posting schedules, and we match them. Delivery timed to your start date, staging at Cox Creek if housing lags, and itemized paperwork for reimbursement programs.

Shipping something with music history? The Shoals moves more storied vehicles than towns ten times its size — restored classics, touring rigs, studio vans. Those ship enclosed with full photo documentation, handled like the history they carry.

W.C. Handy Festival week each July fills downtown Florence. Pickups that week shift to the Cox Creek corridor automatically — no cost, no hassle, just planning.

Killen, Rogersville, and St. Florian ship at Florence prices. Lake-area and rural addresses meet the driver at the nearest wide highway junction to keep loading safe and quick.

The cheap months are late January through February and again in early fall. If your move can wait for a quiet window, Shoals prices drop to their floor and the pickup windows shorten too.

Local Questions

Florence Auto Transport FAQs

Why does pickup take longer in Florence than in Huntsville?
No interstate runs through the Shoals, so carriers group their stops here into passes — that's the honest reason for the 3-to-5-day window. Prices stay within a few percent of Huntsville's, because the route sets the rate.
Should I drive my car to Athens or Decatur to ship it cheaper?
No. It saves nothing — the price keys off the route from the Shoals, not your distance from I-65. Book from your driveway, take the real window, and keep your Saturday.
How do UNA student shipments work?
We ship them every August and May. Students meet the driver at Cox Creek, ten minutes from campus, and we text them the time and spot directly. Parents get confirmation when the car is loaded or delivered.
Are Muscle Shoals, Sheffield, and Tuscumbia the same price as Florence?
Yes — all four cities ship as one market at one rate. Each has its own standard meeting corner, and telling us you can meet on either side of the river often speeds up your pickup by a day.
What does Florence to Nashville cost?
About $350 to $500 for a standard car. At 120 miles it's the Shoals' busiest route, running weekly in both directions with the music-city connection keeping it warm.
Can you ship a classic or a music-history vehicle?
Yes, and we treat those with respect — enclosed trailers, soft straps, and full photo documentation before loading. The Shoals ships more storied vehicles than most cities twice its size.
Do TVA and river-industry relocations ship through you?
Regularly. We time delivery to your posting date, stage at Cox Creek if your housing isn't ready, and supply itemized invoices that reimbursement programs accept.
How fast is delivery once my car is on the truck?
Normal speed — the Shoals difference is only in the pickup window. Nashville and Birmingham arrive in a day or two once rolling; Dallas takes about three days.
Is my car insured during the trip?
Yes. Every carrier is FMCSA-licensed with cargo insurance. The pickup condition form is checked again at delivery, and anything in between is the carrier's responsibility, not yours.
What if my car doesn't run?
We can ship it with winch loading, which adds about $150 to $250. Tell us whether it rolls, steers, and brakes when you book so the right equipment shows up the first time.
When is the cheapest time to ship from the Shoals?
Late January through February, and early fall. Quiet-season prices hit their floor and pickup windows tighten up. Summer and UNA move-in weeks cost the most.
Do you cover Killen, Rogersville, and the lake communities?
Yes, at Florence rates. Lake and rural addresses meet the driver at the nearest wide-shouldered junction, which keeps loading safe and your schedule intact.
How do I prepare my car for shipping?
Clear out personal items, leave about a quarter tank of gas, shut off the alarm, and wash the car so its condition photographs clearly. Bring keys and ID to the meetup.
Can you ship a car for an international UNA student who just bought one?
Yes. We pick up from the seller's city, document the car's condition for the buyer's protection, and deliver to the Cox Creek meetup on the student's schedule. Payment and title stay between buyer and seller — we just move the car safely.
Which Shoals city gets its pickup first?
Whichever side of the river the carrier's grouped route reaches first — it changes load to load. That's exactly why flexibility helps: customers who can meet on either side regularly get picked up a day sooner.
