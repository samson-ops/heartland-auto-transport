How Car Pickup Works in Dothan

Car haulers are long trucks, and the Ross Clark Circle at rush hour is slow going for anything that size. So drivers meet customers at big lots just off the Circle — the Wiregrass Commons area is the favorite — or along US-231 and US-431 where the shoulders are wide. In most neighborhoods, the driver can come close to your door. He'll call about an hour ahead to set the exact spot, and loading takes around 20 minutes.

These are open-transport prices — the standard way cars ship. Your vehicle rides an open trailer with several others, the same way new cars reach dealerships. Panama City and Tallahassee often deliver same-day. Atlanta and Birmingham take a day or two. Miami runs about three days. Enclosed transport, with the car inside a covered trailer, is available for classics and luxury vehicles at 60 to 80 percent more.

What changes the price? Vehicle size first: trucks and big SUVs cost $75 to $150 more than sedans. Season second: summer PCS moves and fall snowbird traffic push prices up, while late winter is the cheapest stretch of the year. Condition third: a car that won't start needs a winch, which adds $150 to $250. Tell Heartland Auto Transport all three things when you get your quote, and the number you see is the number you pay.

Tips for Shipping a Car in Dothan

Military family at Fort Novosel? Book the day your orders drop. Flight school classes graduate together, which means dozens of families ship cars the same weeks. Early booking beats your own class to the carriers. We stage pickups along US-84 toward the Daleville gate and time everything to your report date.

If the Army changes your orders — and it will — your pickup moves with them at no charge. Our dispatchers handle date changes for Novosel families every single week. Keep your invoice and the driver's condition form; those are the two papers the military reimbursement system asks for.

Beach place in Panama City? The 85-mile run is the cheapest saltwater route in Alabama shipping. Lots of Dothan families ship a golf cart or second car down each spring and bring it back each fall. Booking the round trip at once costs less than two separate orders.

New job at Southeast Health or Flowers Hospital? We deliver your car during your first week in town. If your housing isn't ready, we'll meet you at a lot near the Circle and hand off the car wherever you're staying.

Farm equipment ships too. Tractors, sprayers, and UTVs ride flatbed trucks, priced by size and weight. For farm pickups, we just need the machine on firm ground where the trailer's ramps can reach it.

Peanut Festival week in early November clogs the Circle for about two weeks. Pickups that week shift to the US-84 corridor — a small change we make automatically, at no cost to you.

Headland, Rehobeth, Taylor, and Cowarts all ship at Dothan prices. Rural addresses meet the driver at the nearest highway junction, which keeps even far-out pickups on schedule.

The cheapest months here are late January through February. If your move can wait for winter, you'll save real money — carriers drop prices when the summer and snowbird rushes end.

Dothan Auto Transport FAQs

How do Fort Novosel military moves from Dothan work?

Can you ship my golf cart to the beach and back each year?
Yes, and lots of Dothan families do exactly that. Book the spring trip down and the fall trip back together, and the pair costs less than two separate bookings.
Do you move farm equipment in the Wiregrass?
Yes. Tractors, sprayers, and UTVs ride flatbeds, priced by size and weight. Have the machine on firm ground for loading, and tell us if it doesn't run so the right truck comes.
How fast can you pick up a car in Dothan?

Summer PCS season and flight-school graduation weeks lead, with the fall beach traffic close behind. Book a week or two ahead in those windows. Late winter is the cheap, easy season.
