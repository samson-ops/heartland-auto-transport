Montgomery's spot where I-65 meets I-85 puts it on two of the Southeast's busiest carrier lanes. Maxwell Air Force Base and Gunter Annex generate year-round PCS traffic, the Hyundai plant on the south side keeps industrial relocations flowing, and state government churns professional moves every legislative cycle. Atlanta is 160 miles up I-85; the Gulf beaches are two hours south — both lanes run daily.

Local Know-How

Where Carriers Load in Montgomery

EastChase and the Taylor Road corridor are the most carrier-friendly staging areas in town, with Zelda Road and Eastern Boulevard lots as backups. Downtown pickups near the Capitol usually shift to a wider lot a few blocks out. For Maxwell AFB and Gunter moves, drivers meet outside the gates — typically along Maxwell Boulevard or at a Day Street retail lot.

Local Questions

Montgomery Auto Transport FAQs

Can you coordinate a PCS from Maxwell Air Force Base?
Yes — Maxwell and Gunter moves are routine for us. Carriers stage outside the gates, usually on Maxwell Boulevard, and we time pickup to your orders. If your report date shifts, we adjust the window at no charge.
What does Montgomery to Atlanta cost?
About 160 miles up I-85 — one of our most-traveled lanes. A standard sedan on an open carrier typically runs $350–$500 with 1–2 day delivery.
Do you serve Pike Road?
Yes. Pike Road's growth has made it a regular stop. Newer subdivisions with narrow entries usually meet the carrier at the Highway 231 or Vaughn Road retail corridors.
