Local Questions

Vestavia Hills Auto Transport FAQs

Can a car hauler drive up to my house in Vestavia?
On the crest and older mountain streets, no — the grades and turns are beyond what a loaded 10-car trailer can safely manage. Drivers stage at US-31 or Rocky Ridge lots instead, which keeps loading safe and usually saves you time.
Where does Liberty Park meet the carrier?
At the Liberty Parkway retail corner just off I-459. It's two minutes from the neighborhood entrance and gives the driver a straight interstate shot in and out.
Does the staged pickup cost extra?
No. Meeting at a nearby lot is standard practice across over-the-mountain communities and doesn't change your door-to-door rate.
