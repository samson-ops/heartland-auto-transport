Local Insight

Tips for Shipping a Car in Alabaster

Summer moves need a head start. Thompson-zone families all close on houses in the same eight weekends of June and July, and carriers fill up around them. Booking one to two weeks ahead gets normal prices; last-minute summer bookings pay extra for the scramble.

Sending a student to college? Book about three weeks before move-in. We arrange the drop-off time and place directly with your student by text, and the car can land a day after they do so someone's there to meet it.

Selling a car to an out-of-state buyer? Shipping protects both sides. The signed condition form shows the buyer exactly what left your driveway, the carrier's insurance covers the trip, and many sellers roll the shipping cost into a delivered price to reach more buyers.

Moving in before your house closes? We can hold the handoff a day or two if your closing slips — that happens a lot in this market — or meet you at the Promenade and deliver the car wherever you're staying.

Two cars? One truck. Families shipping both vehicles together save 10 to 20 percent per car, with one pickup, one delivery, and one bill.

Saginaw, Simmsville, and the county roads east of the interstate all ship at Alabaster prices. Rural pickups meet along the Highway 31 frontage where the trailer can pull through without backing up.

Trucks and lifted vehicles: tell us the height. Anything taller than stock may need a specific spot on the trailer, and knowing at quote time keeps the price you see accurate.

Winter is the deal season. If your dates can slide to late January or February, carriers have open space and price it to fill — the same route that costs $500 in June often runs $400 in February.

Local Questions

Alabaster Auto Transport FAQs

How fast can you pick up a car in Alabaster?
Usually 1 to 3 business days. I-65 carriers pass by constantly and add Alabaster stops without leaving their route. Summer weekends book out further, so reserve a week or two ahead in June and July.
Is Alabaster priced the same as Birmingham?
Yes. The route sets the price, and Alabaster sits on the same corridor. You pay Birmingham-metro rates without the Birmingham traffic.
What does Alabaster to Atlanta cost?
About $350 to $525 for a standard car — 165 miles with carriers running daily. Delivery in a day or two is normal.
Can you ship my student's car to their college?
Yes, every August and May. We set the time and place with your student directly by text, and the car can arrive right after they do. Book about three weeks before move-in.
Do bigger vehicles really cost more?
Yes — a Tahoe, Suburban, or F-150 runs $75 to $150 more than a sedan because it takes more room and weight on the trailer. Lifted trucks should mention their height at quote time.
Can you ship a car I sold to someone out of state?
Yes, and it protects the sale. The condition form documents what the buyer's getting, insured transit covers the miles, and the delivery signature closes it out cleanly for both sides.
What if my closing date slips?
We flex with it. A day or two of delay is normal in Shelby County's market — the driver can hold the handoff or meet you at the exit 238 lots and deliver wherever you're staying.
Do you pick up in Saginaw and on the county roads?
Yes, at Alabaster rates. Rural pickups meet along the Highway 31 frontage where a loaded trailer has room to work, which keeps even far-out addresses on schedule.
Is my car insured on the truck?
Yes. Every carrier Heartland Auto Transport books is FMCSA-licensed and carries cargo insurance. The signed condition form from pickup is your proof — check the car against it at delivery.
How do I get the car ready?
Empty your personal things, leave about a quarter tank of gas, turn off the alarm and toll tag, and wash it so the photos show its real condition. Have keys ready at the meetup.
What if the car doesn't run?
We ship non-runners with winch loading for an extra $150 to $250. Tell us at booking whether it rolls, steers, and brakes so the right truck comes the first time.
What's the cheapest month to ship from Alabaster?
February, with late January right behind. Winter's open trailer space gets priced to move. Summer school-zone season is the most expensive stretch of the year.
Can you ship two family cars at once?
Yes, and together is cheaper — both vehicles on one truck save 10 to 20 percent each, with a single pickup and delivery. Most Shelby County families moving in summer ship the pair, and one dispatcher handles the whole set from booking to handoff.
Do you deliver cars bought from out-of-state dealers to Alabaster?
Yes. We coordinate with the dealer's delivery desk, pick up once the release paperwork clears, and bring the car to your driveway or the exit 238 meetup — usually within a couple of days for regional purchases.
