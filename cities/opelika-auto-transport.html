Opelika Auto Transport FAQs

Why do carriers prefer Tiger Town for the whole metro?
Space and speed. The lots are huge, exit 62 is right there, and a driver can load and return to I-85 in under fifteen minutes. Both Auburn and Opelika customers benefit from the shared staging point.
Do you handle industrial relocations to the Opelika parks?
Yes — manufacturing hires relocating for the Northeast Industrial Park employers are regular customers. We coordinate pickup at origin around your production start date and stage delivery at Fox Run or Tiger Town.
What does Opelika to Atlanta cost?
Roughly 105 miles up I-85 — typically $300–$450 with next-day delivery on this high-frequency lane.

Inside your onboarding window — production-start timing gets scheduling priority, and the exit-62 staging means dispatch moves fast. Book at offer acceptance, and if orientation shifts with the line schedule, the pickup shifts free. Employer reimbursement formats come standard.
