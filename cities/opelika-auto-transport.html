Opelika's rhythm is steadier than Auburn's, though — outside those waves, exit 62 loads dispatch fast at floor prices most of the year.

How fast can my car follow me to a new industrial park job?

Almost certainly. Our itemized invoice fits company reimbursement programs, and direct billing runs where the employer allows it. Send the program details with your quote request.

About $300 to $450 — 105 miles up I-85 on a corridor carriers never leave. Next-day delivery is normal, and flexible dates catch the low end.

Off the narrow blocks. Railroad-district addresses meet on Second Avenue's wider stretches or at Tiger Town, minutes away either way. The revival blocks stay beautiful and the loading stays easy.

Is my car insured during transport?
Yes. Every carrier holds FMCSA licensing and cargo insurance. The signed pickup condition record is your protection — verify against it at delivery.

Personal items out, about a quarter tank in, alarm and toll tag off, and a quick wash so the photos read true. Keys and ID at the handoff.

Most of the year, honestly. Outside plant hiring waves and the August and May student rushes, exit 62 loads dispatch at floor prices with next-day speed.

Do you ship motorcycles from Opelika?
