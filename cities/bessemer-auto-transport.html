Local Questions

Bessemer Auto Transport FAQs

Can you bill my employer for a relocation to the Bessemer fulfillment center?
If their program allows direct billing, yes — we set it up with the relocation coordinator and you never touch the invoice. Otherwise you get the itemized receipt and signed condition form that reimbursement programs require.
How fast can you really pick up in Bessemer?
Same-week is routine, and 1 to 2 days is common. The I-20/59 corridor's constant traffic means a truck with an open spot is almost always nearby — even in seasons when quieter suburbs wait.
Can you bring my auction car back from Atlanta or Dallas?
Yes — both routes pass through Bessemer daily. Send the auction's release requirements and your buyer credentials, and we dispatch inside the free-storage window. Non-running wins need the winch declared up front.
Do you handle track cars near Barber Motorsports?
Yes — enclosed trailers with liftgate loading for low clearance, plus drivers who respect race-prep tie-down points. Tell us the ride height and strap preferences at booking and the right rig shows up.
What does Bessemer to Dallas cost?
About $600 to $850 for a standard car, roughly three days door to door. Westbound I-20 traffic keeps this one of the best-priced long routes in Alabama.
Where do apartment residents near the warehouses meet the driver?
At the Academy Drive or Morgan Road retail corners, two minutes from every complex on that stretch. Complexes bar big trucks from their lots, and the corners load faster anyway.
Do seasonal workers really ship cars here for a few months?
More every year. A fall-push posting with a car shipped both ways beats four months of highway miles. Round trips booked as a pair price below two singles, and we hold the return window through the season.
Is Hueytown covered at Bessemer prices?
The border neighborhoods, yes — the southwest metro prices as one market. Deeper Hueytown addresses stage along Allison-Bonnett, and your quote keys off the route either way.
Is my car insured while it ships?
Yes. Between your pickup signature and your delivery signature, the carrier's FMCSA-required cargo insurance covers the car. The condition form is your evidence — check against it before signing at delivery.
What if the car won't start?
Winch loading handles it for $150 to $250 extra. If it won't roll at all, we arrange a rollback instead. Tell us the truth about what it can do and the schedule holds.
How do I prep the car?
Personal items out, about a quarter tank in, alarm and toll tag off, and a quick wash so the condition photos are clear. Keys and ID at the handoff — that's the whole list.
When is the cheapest time to ship from Bessemer?
Mid-winter and mid-spring, between the hiring ramp and summer. Flexible dates in those windows catch corridor pricing at its floor.
Can two vehicles from one household share a truck?
Yes, and it's the standard money-saver — 10 to 20 percent off each car when they ride together, with one pickup and one delivery. McCalla families moving in summer do it constantly, and one dispatcher owns the whole booking.
What does Bessemer to Atlanta cost?
About $350 to $525 for a standard car, usually delivered in a day or two. Eastbound I-20 traffic keeps this route liquid year-round, and flexible dates catch the bottom of the range.
Do you ship motorcycles from Bessemer?
Yes — bikes ride wheel-chocked on open carriers or crated in enclosed trailers for the valuable ones, priced well under car rates. The Barber scene keeps this route busier than most towns our size.
