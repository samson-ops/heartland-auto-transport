These are open-transport prices, the standard way nearly every car ships. Your vehicle rides an open trailer with several others, exactly how dealerships receive new cars. Nashville and Birmingham often deliver next day. Memphis and Atlanta take a day or two, Chicago about three. For classics and luxury cars, enclosed transport inside a covered trailer runs 60 to 80 percent more.

Your price moves with three things. Size: pickups and big SUVs cost $75 to $150 more than sedans because they fill more trailer space. Season: summer family moves and plant hiring waves raise prices, while late winter drops them. Condition: a car that won't start needs winch loading, adding $150 to $250. Flexible dates are the easy discount — give the carrier a three-day pickup window instead of one fixed day and you'll usually save $50 to $100.

Local Insight

Tips for Shipping a Car in Decatur

Relocating for 3M, Nucor, or ULA? Book the day you accept the offer. When a plant hires a class or runs a big project, the surrounding weeks fill with inbound moves. We deliver your car during your first week and can stage it at exit 334 until your housing is ready.

Keep your paperwork if your company reimburses moves. Our itemized invoice and the driver's condition form are exactly what corporate relocation programs ask for, and we can bill some employers directly.

Schedule pickups mid-morning or early afternoon. Shift change stacks the river bridges twice a day, and a pickup that dodges those windows takes twenty minutes instead of an hour.

The Nashville run is the bargain here — 100 miles straight north, usually $300 to $450, often next-day. If you're weighing shipping against a round-trip drive with a friend, the math almost always favors shipping.

Hartselle, Priceville, and Trinity all ship at Decatur prices. Rural Morgan County addresses meet the driver at the nearest highway junction, which keeps every pickup on schedule.

Sending a student to college? August and May are the big months, and we text the student directly to set the handoff. Book three weeks before move-in for the best rates.

Shipping a UTV or a jon-boat tow rig? The tow vehicle ships standard, and UTVs ride flatbeds priced by size. Seasonal round trips booked together cost less than two separate orders.

Late January through February is Decatur's cheap season. Carriers have empty spots after the holidays and price them to move. If your dates can slide to winter, slide them.

Local Questions

Decatur Auto Transport FAQs

How fast can you pick up a car in Decatur?
Usually 1 to 3 business days. Decatur draws from two carrier pools at once — I-65 through-traffic and Huntsville's metro fleet — so even busy weeks rarely slow it much.
Do you handle 3M, Nucor, and ULA relocations?
Every month. We time delivery to your start date, stage the car at exit 334 if housing isn't settled, and provide the itemized paperwork company relocation programs want. Some employers can be billed directly.
What does Decatur to Nashville cost?
About $300 to $450 for a standard car — 100 miles up one of the busiest carrier corridors in the South. Next-day delivery is normal, and flexible dates catch the low end.
When is the best time of day for a Decatur pickup?
Mid-morning through early afternoon. The river bridges jam at plant shift change — roughly 6:30 to 7:30 a.m. and 3 to 4 p.m. — so drivers schedule between those windows and loading stays quick.
Is Hartselle covered at Decatur prices?
Yes. Hartselle, Priceville, Trinity, and the rest of Morgan County all quote at the same rate. Your price is set by the route, not your exit number.
Can you ship my college student's car?
Yes — August and May are our big student months. We coordinate directly with your student by text and meet at a lot near campus on the other end. Book about three weeks before move-in week.
What if my car doesn't run?
We ship non-runners regularly. The driver brings a winch, which adds about $150 to $250. Tell us at booking whether it rolls, steers, and brakes so the right truck arrives.
Is my car insured on the truck?
Yes. Every carrier Heartland Auto Transport dispatches is FMCSA-licensed with cargo insurance. The condition form from pickup is your protection — check the car against it at delivery before signing.
Can you move a UTV or boat tow rig?
Yes. Tow vehicles ship like any car, and UTVs ride flatbeds priced by footprint. Book seasonal round trips together and the pair costs less than two singles.
How do I get my car ready?
Take out personal items, leave a quarter tank of gas, turn off the alarm and toll tag, and rinse off the dirt so condition photos are clear. Bring keys and ID to the handoff.
Can you hold delivery until my house closes?
Yes. We stage cars at exit 334 or the Beltline and hand off when your keys are ready. A day or two of flex usually costs nothing.
What's the cheapest month to ship from Decatur?
February, with late January close behind. Carriers cut prices after the holidays to fill trucks. Summer is the priciest season, so flexible movers save by aiming at winter.
Do you serve Mooresville and the river-north communities?
Yes, at Decatur rates. When bridge traffic is heavy, the driver stages on the north bank along the Mooresville Road corridor so your handoff stays a quick stop.
Can two plant families share one truck on the same route?
Yes, when postings line up — and it's the best discount going. Two households transferring between the same plant cities the same week ride one carrier and each save 10 to 20 percent per vehicle. The river plants move people in groups often enough that matches happen more than you'd think, so tell dispatch about your cohort.
Do you ship EVs from Decatur?
Yes, on the same trucks as gas cars. Charge the battery to between 30 and 80 percent, tell us the model, and the driver handles transport mode at loading. That's the only difference.
