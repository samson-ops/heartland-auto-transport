Auburn runs on the university calendar, and so does car shipping here. Every August, student cars arrive from Texas, Georgia, Florida, and cities all over the country. Every May, they head home. Heartland Auto Transport moves hundreds of these cars each year, plus vehicles for professors, staff, and families across Lee County. Auburn sits right on I-85 between Atlanta and Montgomery, so carriers pass by every day and prices stay fair.

For parents, the best part is how hands-off it is. You book the shipment and pay. We handle the rest with your student directly — we text them the meeting time and place, they show up with the keys, and the driver does a walk-around inspection before loading. You get a message when it's done. No flying down, no two-car caravan, no missed work.

Local Know-How

How Car Pickup Works in Auburn

Campus streets and student apartment complexes can't fit a 75-foot car hauler, and most complexes ban big trucks anyway. So student cars load at Tiger Town in Opelika or the South College Street shopping centers — both about ten minutes from campus. For houses and neighborhoods around town, the driver often comes right to your driveway. If your street is tight, he'll pick a nearby lot and call you an hour ahead with the spot.

Local Insight

Tips for Shipping a Car in Auburn

Book early for August. The move-in window from August 1 to 20 is one of the busiest shipping periods in the whole Southeast. Families who book three to four weeks ahead get their pick of dates at normal prices. Families who wait pay rush prices for whatever's left.

Game days are real. On the seven home football Saturdays, roads close and the whole area jams — and Friday afternoons before games aren't much better in October. If your dates land near a game weekend, we'll shift the pickup to earlier in that week at no charge.

December is the sleeper deal. Students who graduate in December or leave for study abroad ship home at some of the best prices of the year. Carriers want loads after Thanksgiving, and Heartland Auto Transport passes those low rates straight to you.

Professors and staff, this works for you too. Auburn hires from all over the country, and we deliver cars timed to semester start dates. If the university gave you a moving allowance, our itemized invoice is exactly what the reimbursement office wants.

Moving to a retirement community east of town? We ship golf carts too. They cost much less than cars, and we coordinate with the gatehouse ahead of time so the driver gets in without a hitch.

Opelika counts as Auburn. The two cities share one shipping market, one set of prices, and one main meeting spot at Tiger Town. If you have a car in each city, we can handle both in a single booking.

Not sure whether to ship or drive? Do the math. Gas, hotels, meals, and two days of your time on a Dallas-to-Auburn drive usually add up to more than the shipping price — and your car arrives with zero new miles on it.

Get the car ready the simple way: clear out personal items, leave about a quarter tank of gas, turn off the alarm, and pull the parking decal if it's expired. A quick wash helps the inspection photos show the car's true condition.

Local Questions

Auburn Auto Transport FAQs

When should I book my student's car for August move-in?
Three to four weeks ahead. The August 1–20 window is the busiest of the year, and early bookings get normal prices and first choice of dates. Waiting until the last week usually means paying rush rates.
Do I need to be in Auburn for the pickup or delivery?
No. We coordinate directly with your student by text — time, place, and what to bring. They meet the driver at Tiger Town or South College, sign the condition form, and you get confirmation when it's done.
Where exactly does my student meet the driver?
Usually Tiger Town in Opelika or a South College Street shopping center, about ten minutes from campus. Campus streets and apartment complexes can't fit the truck, so these big lots are the standard meetup spots.
How much is shipping from Dallas or Houston to Auburn?
Roughly $700 to $950 for a standard car. Texas sends Auburn one of its biggest out-of-state student groups, so the route runs constantly. Two families from the same city sharing a truck each save 10 to 20 percent.
What happens if my dates land on a football weekend?
We move the pickup earlier in that week for free. Game-day traffic makes loading nearly impossible, so we plan around all seven home Saturdays automatically.
Can two families really share one truck?
Yes, and it's the best discount in Auburn. If both cars start in the same metro area and ship the same week, they ride together and each family saves 10 to 20 percent. Tell us the hometown and we'll check for a match.
Do you ship cars for Auburn faculty and staff?
Yes. We time delivery to your semester start date, and our itemized invoice works with university moving allowances. New hires from across the country use us every year.
Is my car insured on the truck?
Yes. Every carrier Heartland Auto Transport uses is FMCSA-licensed and insured. The driver records the car's condition at pickup, your student or you verify it at delivery, and anything in between is covered.
Can you ship a car home in December?
Yes, and it's a bargain month. December graduates and study-abroad students get some of the year's lowest rates. We pick up after finals, before the apartments close for break.
What does Auburn to Atlanta cost?
About $300 to $450. It's 110 miles up I-85 with carriers passing daily, so next-day delivery is normal and prices stay low year-round.
How does my student prepare the car?
Empty out personal items, leave a quarter tank of gas, turn off the alarm, and bring the keys and a photo ID to the meetup. The driver handles the rest, including the walk-around inspection.
Do you ship golf carts to Auburn communities?
Yes. Golf carts cost well under car rates and ride strapped on the same trucks. For gated communities, we arrange gate access ahead of the delivery day.
