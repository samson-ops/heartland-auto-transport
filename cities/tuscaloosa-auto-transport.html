Auto Transport in Tuscaloosa, Alabama

Ship your car to or from Tuscaloosa — student move-ins, Mercedes plant relocations, and family moves, all planned around game days.

Tuscaloosa ships a lot of cars for a city its size, and two things drive it: the University of Alabama and the Mercedes-Benz plant. Every August, thousands of student cars arrive from Texas, Georgia, Tennessee, and beyond. Every May, they head home. Year-round, the Mercedes plant in nearby Vance brings workers and their vehicles from across the country. Tuscaloosa sits right on I-20/59, so carriers pass through daily and prices stay fair.

Shipping is easier than driving a car across the country yourself. Use the form above for a free quote, or give us a call. We book a licensed, insured carrier. The driver meets you, inspects the car with you, and writes down its condition. The car rides the truck while you get updates, and at delivery you check it over and sign. For students, we text them directly to set up the handoff — parents don't have to play messenger.

Local Know-How

How Car Pickup Works in Tuscaloosa

Campus streets and student apartment complexes can't fit a 75-foot car hauler, and most complexes don't allow big trucks anyway. So student pickups happen at big lots nearby — usually University Mall or the Shoppes at Legacy Park, about ten minutes from the dorms. For everyone else, most driveways and neighborhoods work for door-to-door service. When they don't, the driver picks a lot on McFarland or Skyland Boulevard. The driver calls about an hour before to confirm the spot.

What changes the price? Size first: trucks and big SUVs cost $75 to $150 more than sedans. One money-saving trick that works especially well in Tuscaloosa: if two families from the same hometown are shipping to campus the same week, both cars can share a truck and each family saves 10 to 20 percent.

Local Insight

Tips for Shipping a Car in Tuscaloosa

Book student shipments three to four weeks before move-in. August 1 through 20 is the busiest shipping window in Alabama, and carriers fill up fast. Early booking gets you a better price and your choice of dates. May move-out is nearly as busy — same rule applies.

Football Saturdays change everything. On the seven home game weekends, roads close, traffic stops, and loading a car becomes nearly impossible. We schedule around those dates automatically. If your move lands on a game weekend, we'll suggest the Tuesday or Wednesday before — no extra charge, way less stress.

Moving for the Mercedes plant? We move plant and supplier employees all the time. We time delivery to your start date, provide the paperwork your company's moving program needs, and can meet you near Vance or your new neighborhood.

Parents: you don't need to be in Tuscaloosa for the handoff. We text your student directly with the pickup or delivery time and meet them at University Mall or Legacy Park between classes. You get a confirmation when it's done.

Same-hometown savings are real here. Alabama draws big groups of students from Dallas, Houston, Atlanta, and Nashville. If your student's car ships the same week as a classmate's from the same city, both ride one truck and both families save. Tell us the hometown and we'll look for a match.

Live in Northport? Same prices, and sometimes faster pickups. Carriers coming from Memphis or Mississippi hit the Northport side of the river first. On game weekends, meeting on the Northport side keeps your shipment moving when campus-side loading shuts down.

December is a hidden bargain. Students studying abroad in spring or graduating in December ship home at some of the year's lowest prices, because carriers are hungry for loads after Thanksgiving.

Get the car ready the easy way: remove personal items, leave a quarter tank of gas, kill the alarm, and take your parking pass off the mirror. Wash it so the condition photos are clear. That's the whole checklist.

Local Questions

Tuscaloosa Auto Transport FAQs

When should I book my student's car shipment to Alabama?
Three to four weeks before August move-in. That window is the busiest of the year, and early booking gets better prices and your choice of dates. May move-out needs the same head start.
Where do students meet the driver?
At University Mall or the Shoppes at Legacy Park, about ten minutes from campus. Campus streets and apartment complexes can't fit the truck. We text your student the time and spot directly.
What happens on football weekends?
Loading near campus becomes nearly impossible on the seven home Saturdays, so we schedule around them — usually the Tuesday or Wednesday before. It costs nothing extra and saves a lot of stress.
How much does it cost to ship a car from Dallas to Tuscaloosa?
About $600 to $850 for a standard car, around 580 miles. Texas is Alabama's biggest out-of-state student group, so this route runs constantly. Two families from the same city can share a truck and each save 10 to 20 percent.
Do you handle Mercedes plant relocations?
Yes, weekly. We time delivery to your start date, provide paperwork for company moving programs, and cover Vance and the whole plant corridor at Tuscaloosa rates.
Is Northport the same price as Tuscaloosa?
Yes — both sides of the river ship as one market. Northport can even be faster on game weekends, when campus-side loading shuts down but the north bank stays open.
Can my student's car ship home in December?
Yes, and it's a bargain month. December graduates and study-abroad students ship home at some of the year's best prices. We pick up after finals and before the dorms close.
Does my student need to do anything special at the handoff?
Just show up with ID and the keys, walk around the car with the driver, and sign the condition form. It takes about 20 minutes. We text them the time and location, and you get confirmation when it's done.
Is the car insured during the trip?
Yes. Every carrier is FMCSA-licensed and insured. The condition form from pickup is checked again at delivery, and the carrier's insurance covers anything that happened in between.
What if my car doesn't run?
We can ship it. The driver brings a winch, which adds about $150 to $250. Tell us when you book whether the car rolls, steers, and brakes so the right truck shows up.
What's the cheapest way to ship two cars to Tuscaloosa?
On the same truck. Two cars from one family — or two families from the same hometown — sharing a carrier saves 10 to 20 percent per car. Tell us both vehicles when you get your quote.
Can faculty and staff moving to the University use you too?
Yes. We move professors and staff hired from across the country, timed to semester start dates. University moving allowances usually reimburse against our itemized invoice — keep it with your offer paperwork.
How far ahead should regular (non-student) moves book?
One to two weeks is plenty most of the year. Just avoid booking into August 1–20, early May, and home football weekends without extra lead time — those windows fill up first.
