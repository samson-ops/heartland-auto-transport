Most of those new neighbors arrive from somewhere else — Michigan, Ohio, Texas — and their cars arrive with Heartland Auto Transport. Never shipped a car? It takes three steps. One: get a free quote above or by phone. How Car Pickup Works in Athens

Birmingham and Huntsville dealers work the same way. Limestone County buyers increasingly purchase from whichever metro has the right car and ship it home for a few hundred dollars instead of burning a Saturday on a dealer shuttle.

New subdivision? Just tell us the neighborhood name. We confirm which entrance is paved and where a trailer can stage before dispatch, so the driver arrives ready instead of circling.

Late January through February is bargain season here like everywhere on I-65. Carriers price winter space to move, and flexible Athens shippers catch the same route $75 to $100 under its summer price.

Local Questions

Athens Auto Transport FAQs

How fast can you pick up a car in Athens?
Usually 1 to 2 business days — Athens sits directly on the I-65 corridor carriers already run. Plant onboarding weeks stretch it slightly, which is why booking at offer acceptance beats booking at packing time.
I'm relocating for the Mazda Toyota plant — how should I time the car?
Book when you accept the offer. Hiring classes cluster dozens of moves into the same weeks, and early bookings choose carriers at normal prices. We deliver during your arrival week and hold the car at exit 351 if housing paperwork runs long.
Is it cheaper to ship from Huntsville than Athens?
No — identical prices. Both sit on the same corridor and quote off the same route rates. Book from your own driveway and skip the pointless repositioning drive.
What does Athens to Nashville cost?
About $300 to $425, often delivered same-day or next-day. At 95 miles on the busiest carrier corridor in the region, it's genuinely one of the cheapest interstate shipping routes in the country.
Can you bring me a car I bought from a Nashville or Birmingham dealer?
Yes, usually within two days of the release paperwork clearing. We coordinate with the dealer's delivery desk, the driver inspects and loads at the lot, and the car arrives with the window sticker still on.
My subdivision is brand new — will the driver find it?
Yes, because we confirm the details first: which entrance is paved, where construction gates sit, whether the entrance can stage a trailer. Athens' newest streets ship as smoothly as the square.
Can you move a UTV or compact tractor?
Yes — small equipment rides flatbeds priced by footprint and weight. Rural pickups meet at the nearest highway junction on firm ground, and non-runners need the winch declared at booking.
Do you serve Elkmont and the northern county?
Yes, at Athens rates. Northern Limestone meets at the exit 361 corridor or the closest numbered highway. The route sets your price, not your distance from town.
What's the best time of day for pickup?
Mid-morning through early afternoon. The county's two-lane roads stack with school traffic from 7 to 8 a.m. and 2:30 to 4 p.m., and a pickup between those windows wraps in about twenty minutes.
Is my car insured on the trip?
Yes. Every carrier is FMCSA-licensed with cargo insurance. Your signed pickup condition form is the record — check the car against it at delivery before signing.
Can you hold delivery until my house closes?
Yes, and in this market we plan for it. A day or two of flex is free, and longer gaps use the exit 351 meetup so you can take the car wherever you're staying.
When is shipping cheapest from Athens?
Late January through February. Winter trailer space gets priced to fill, and the same route that runs $475 in June often runs $375 to $400 then. Flexible dates catch the bottom.
Can you ship my college student's car from Athens?
Yes, every August and May. We text the student the time and place directly and meet at a big lot near campus on the other end. Book about three weeks ahead of move-in week for normal prices and first pick of dates.
Do two households heading the same direction save money?
Yes — when two cars from the Athens area ship to the same metro the same week, they can share one carrier and each save 10 to 20 percent. Plant onboarding classes make these matches surprisingly common, so tell us your timing.
